\begin{abstract}
When a frozen language model writes facts into a LoRA adapter online --- one per turn, single
pass, and then day after day --- what does the adapter actually become? We argue, from per-fact
audits at two timescales, that it becomes a \emph{recognition index}, not a fact store, and that
the system design follows from that identity. \textbf{Act 1 (the store fails):} accumulation is
re-instatement, not persistence --- median time-to-first-miss is 3--8 writes under every mechanism
on the primary substrate (4--18 on the transfer substrate) while
end-of-stream retention varies by $12\times$; naked sequential SGD sustains only a
$\sim$1.5-batch FIFO; across mechanisms paraphrase recall is 12--31\%, and the compositions that
recall $\sim$90--95\% in the write form answer only 21--25\% of paraphrases; and a per-fact savings probe finds \emph{no} accelerated re-acquisition
of forgotten facts. \textbf{Act 2 (recognition survives):} behind the failing recall readout,
protected mechanisms leave most facts 2-AFC-discriminable --- EWC on the second substrate recalls
\emph{nothing} while recognizing well above chance, and a GRACE-style editor at matched budget
recalls 0--1/48 while recognizing 45--47/48. Recognition is the last readout to die, and the
phenotype is cross-mechanism. \textbf{Act 3 (truth must live in the log):} at identical nightly
budget, error-gated consolidation from a ground-truth log retains $\approx$2.1--2.2$\times$ a recency baseline
and rescues exactly the recognized-but-not-recalled class (next-day survival $\approx$0\% $\to$ 46\%);
nights without a log do not merely fail, they \emph{poison} (pure self-recitation 0/144; a hybrid
lands \emph{below} doing nothing, 4.3 vs.\ 25.0); over 30 days no knee is found within range, and
a capability firewall plus a 100-item GSM8K probe scope what serving costs. \textbf{Act 4 (the
surviving recognition is the key to using the log):} a candidate-free adapter-vs-base log-probability
signal on \emph{presented} content separates written from never-written facts at AUC
0.913--0.932 in a question form whose recall is 1--6/48 (cloze 0.977--0.999); the base-only
signal sits at chance (0.489--0.541) by construction and by measurement; and a minimal
gate demo with predictions specified before execution both works (an answer-independent slot-KL gate, in the
cloze form, cuts direct-form fabrication from
$\approx$100\% to 10--33\% at a frozen threshold) and fails informatively (candidate-scored gates
collapse to self-argmax bias). The readout gap --- recognized when shown at AUC 0.93--0.99, hard
to read out unprompted (Youden $J{=}0.27$ on the calibration seed; 0.35--0.50 on held-out seeds)
--- mirrors the human recognition/recall asymmetry,
and we give it a mechanism: a Jacobian-lens probe shows the answer stays present in the model's
verbalizable workspace even when unrecalled (dipping only $21\%$ at first miss, far above matched
nulls, and recovering), so forgetting here is rank displacement in emission, not loss of the
content. \textbf{Act 5 (the design, run):} on unmaintained writes, forgetting is a \emph{ladder} of readout failures
with dissociable half-lives (free recall 2, gold-in-top-10 --- the retrieval-key tier --- 7,
2-AFC 16 fact-writes), and a write-dose sweep shows dose buys acquisition, not capacity:
end-state recognition is pinned at $\approx$0.5 of the stream across an $8\times$ dose and
$30\times$ step-size range, while a \emph{single} step per fact yields the healthiest key tier
and near-zero decay for whatever it acquires; under the full maintained policy even the emission
tier reaches 0.82--0.85 of a 384-fact stream (three seeds; measured by the loop's own
form-matched probe) with a flat-to-inverted age profile ---
a capacity of the log-driven repair loop, not of unmaintained weights (unmaintained, emission
half-life is $\sim$2 writes) --- and forgetting is a property of the write regime, not the
substrate; an adjudication probe locates the one non-commoditized use of the channel ---
deciding between conflicting records, where the frozen base sits at chance --- and an agent
drives the whole loop end-to-end under naive prompting. The design that survives all
five acts: generation
upstream, truth in the log, single-step writes, and the adapter serving as a familiarity organ
over external content --- the model does remember; it just needs its notebook.
A closing external audit on the InMind implicit-association benchmark --- to our knowledge its
first parametric-memory arm --- confirms the division adversarially and then extends it: the
published wall replicates and factors into retrieval $\times$ dilution; channel-by-channel
isolation puts the in-weight \emph{serve-time} contribution at zero; and a
canonical-protocol program (pre-registered arm by arm, with two declared probe amendments)
then moves the wall from the published systems' $0.171$ of the benchmark's published
in-context ceiling to $\mathbf{0.53}$ (natural personal facts, one $9$B substrate, single
judge) ---
with the honest attribution that most of the climb is instrument (genre-matched
first-person queries; short precise contexts; an annealing effect where writes that
\emph{follow} the fact unlock the readout), the write's cashable residue is a
consistent-but-underpowered $+0.048$ as a \emph{query sharpener} under per-task isolation
(its keyword-level pointer, by contrast, is priced at zero once an agentic loop may
simply re-query) --- while under the realistic write stream the write cashes at
conventional significance: $+0.136$ over the best frozen front end (exact $p{=}0.019$),
with the pipeline's two pre-registered gold-free controls (no writes; chatter-only
writes) collapsing to $0.080$ against the arm's $0.448$ --- and a
certification--retrieval dose wall separates what two write-steps certify from what six can
retrieve --- with the index's key shown to be a property of the write format itself.
Everything is reported as floors and directions with per-seed numbers; the instrument, raw
per-fact timelines, and the self-run refutations of our own stronger readings are all released.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Language-model agents increasingly ship with external memory --- files, retrieval, context ---
while their weights stay frozen after deployment. A recurring alternative is to let the agent
\emph{write} to a parameter-efficient adapter online, one interaction at a time, and consolidate
at rest \citep{lee2026offline,behrouz2026sleep,scm2026,behrouz2024titans}. Systematic audits of
LoRA-as-memory exist for the \emph{batch} regime \citep{back2026lora}; the streaming regime that
deployed agents actually face --- one fact per turn, single pass, no i.i.d.\ shuffling, then day
after day --- has had proposals but not measurement.

This paper measures it, per fact, at two timescales, and argues one thesis:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Online in-weight memory is a recognition \emph{index}, not a fact \emph{store} --- and
the system design follows.}
\end{quote}

A concurrent line of work asks, at the level of methods and benchmarks, \emph{when} continual
learning requires learning --- when adaptation must live inside model weights and when external
scaffolding suffices \citep{harrington2026when}. This paper answers that question for one cell of
the taxonomy, declarative fact memory, with per-fact instrumentation: almost never for storage
(the log does that better at matched budget), and yet the weights are not useless --- they carry a
recognition index that storage-centric evaluation never looks for.

The argument runs in five acts, each an audit with its own pre-registered predictions and its own
self-run refutations. \textbf{Act 1} shows the store failing, in a structured way: persistence is
not accumulation (facts flicker below the recall threshold within 3--8 writes under every
mechanism on the primary substrate, 4--18 on the transfer substrate; capacity is set by
re-instatement, not survival); expression is form-bound (across mechanisms paraphrase
recall collapses to 12--31\%; the compositions with write-form recall $\sim$90--95\% answer only
21--25\%); and what forgetting leaves
behind is bounded by our own savings probe to \emph{a residual trace that supports 2-AFC
discrimination but neither expression nor accelerated re-acquisition}. \textbf{Act 2} shows what
survives the wreckage: a large recognized-but-not-recalled class behind every protected write
rule, at its purest 0/48 recalled with 31--38/48 recognized (EWC on the second substrate), and
reproduced by a completely different mechanism --- a GRACE-style key--value editor at matched
budget recalls 0--1/48 while recognizing 45--47/48. Recognition is the last readout to die.
\textbf{Act 3} shows that expressed truth must therefore live outside the weights: error-gated
consolidation from a ground-truth log retains $\approx$2.1--2.2$\times$ a recency baseline at identical budget
and rescues precisely the latent class; consolidating from the model's own recitations does not
merely fail but poisons (a pre-registered hybrid landed \emph{below} doing nothing about the
past); and the loop runs 30 simulated days without finding a knee, behind a verified capability
firewall. \textbf{Act 4} names the surviving signal: the recognition that survives is exactly
what a log-backed system needs. A candidate-free familiarity probe --- the adapter-vs-base
log-probability of \emph{presented} content --- separates written from never-written facts at AUC
0.913--0.932 in a question form whose recall is 1--6/48, scales with memory strength across
adapters, sits at chance for the base model by construction, and supports a minimal
fabrication-cutting gate --- while our own candidate-scored gate attempts fail in an instructive
way that bounds what the signal can do. \textbf{Act 5} runs the design: forgetting on
unmaintained writes resolves into a ladder of readout tiers with dissociable half-lives; a dose
sweep selects single-step writes; the read path, the question form, and model scale are each
audited; and the resulting policy is then driven to length --- a 384-fact maintained stream on a
9B substrate holds cumulative recall 0.815--0.849 (mean 0.828, three seeds) with a flat-to-inverted age curve, an
adjudication probe shows where the in-weight channel is not commoditized, and an agent drives
the whole loop end-to-end.

\paragraph{Contributions.} One per act, plus the instrument they share and a closing external audit.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A de-confounded per-fact instrument at two timescales} (\S\ref{sec:instrument}):
collision-free fact streams into LoRA on a frozen base; greedy-recall and 2-AFC recognition
probes; pinned recovery statistics; matched compute budgets; a capability firewall verified on
all 39 composition runs (both substrates) and holding by construction for adapter-off serving on
the remaining arms; and a complete day/night loop run end-to-end for up to 30 simulated days.
\item \textbf{The store fails, structurally} (\S\ref{sec:act1}): accumulation is re-instatement,
not persistence; expression is form-bound (recognition $\gg$ write-form recall $\gg$ paraphrase
recall); and forgetting under protection leaves a residual trace bounded --- by our own savings
probe --- to 2-AFC discrimination only.
\item \textbf{Recognition survives, cross-mechanism} (\S\ref{sec:act2}): the
recognized-but-not-recalled class appears under protected SGD, under a matched-budget key--value
editor, and across substrates and depths; the depth gradient of online writes is an
\emph{expression} gradient, not a storage gradient.
\item \textbf{Truth must live in the log} (\S\ref{sec:act3}): budget-matched night policies
dissociate (error-gated $\approx$2.1--2.2$\times$ recency; latent-class survival $\approx$0\% $\to$ 46\%); recitation
poisons ($6\times$ worse than doing nothing); no knee within a 30-day horizon; serving costs are
measured and scoped (``not aphasic'' for core-only serving; read-time masking with final-probe
ratios $4.7$--$15\times$ under the two-adapter hot mount).
\item \textbf{The surviving recognition is the key to using the log} (\S\ref{sec:act4}): a
candidate-free familiarity signal at AUC 0.913--0.999 on presented content, a base-only chance
row that rules out generic-uncertainty gating by construction, a minimal gate (predictions specified before execution) that
cuts direct-form fabrication $\approx$100\% $\to$ 10--33\% at a frozen threshold in the cloze
form, an honest negative on
candidate-scored gating (self-argmax bias), and the resulting division of labor: generation
upstream, log as truth store, adapter as recognition organ over \emph{external} content.
\item \textbf{An operating regime and a closed loop} (\S\ref{sec:act5}): a write policy
(single-step writes plus log-driven, error-gated replay), a maintained-regime capacity
measurement --- 384 facts at cumulative recall 0.815--0.849 (three seeds) on a 9B substrate, with a
flat-to-inverted age curve --- an adjudication probe locating the one non-commoditized use of
the in-weight channel, and an agent that drives the loop autonomously under naive prompting.
\item \textbf{A closing external audit} (\S\ref{sec:inmind}): to our knowledge the first parametric-memory arm
on the InMind implicit-association benchmark. The published wall replicates and factors into
retrieval $\times$ dilution; a training-free answer-side loop triples the retrieval layer;
and channel-by-channel isolation measures the in-weight contribution at zero for serving ---
with the write accent a quantified cost --- turning someone else's benchmark into an
adversarial confirmation of the division of labor. A canonical-protocol program
(pre-registered arm by arm) then takes the same benchmark from the published wall to $0.53$
of the benchmark's published in-context ceiling (natural personal facts; single judge),
with attribution controls at every step (the final real-stream arm's pair of write-free
controls both collapse to $0.080$) separating instrument gains from the
write's own (small, consistent) contribution, and closes with a certification--retrieval
dose wall and a demonstration that the index's retrieval key is chosen by the write format.
\end{enumerate}

We present all of this as \emph{floors and directions}, not ceilings and points: a 2B primary
and a 1.7B transfer substrate for the main audits, single-seed scale rungs at 0.5--9B, synthetic
facts, 1--5 seeds per condition on nondeterministic hardware
(\S\ref{sec:limits}).\footnote{Earlier versions of the day-scale and loop audits appeared as
preprints (doi:10.5281/zenodo.21232648, Part~I; doi:10.5281/zenodo.21310038, Part~II); this version restructures them around the index-not-store thesis
and adds Act 4.}

\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\textbf{LoRA as knowledge memory (batch regime).} \citet{back2026lora} audit LoRA as a parametric
memory under multi-epoch injection: capacity scales with rank and saturates; QA-format supervision
dominates; composition is bottlenecked by routing. Their stated next step --- time-incremental
streams --- is the regime studied here, and at least one of their operational conclusions
(early-layer placement) inverts for \emph{recall} in it (\S\ref{sec:act2},
Appendix~\ref{app:depth}). \citet{xu2026lora} derive a parametric memory law for LoRA retention
--- the closest methodological prior --- again in the batch regime, without a recognition channel
or per-fact timelines. \citet{substrate2026} report that LoRA stores style well and facts poorly
--- independent support, from the batch side, for the substrate asymmetry our streaming audit
measures per fact. The knowledge-injection comparisons reach the same prescription from the
task side: retrieval beats fine-tuning for injecting facts \citep{ovadia2023injection}, and
fine-tuning on new knowledge teaches hallucination faster than it teaches the knowledge
\citep{gekhman2024finetuning}. What those batch-level verdicts lack is the per-fact,
per-write mechanism --- which readout dies first, what survives, and what the surviving tier
is good for --- which is this paper's subject.

\textbf{Lifelong knowledge editing.} WISE \citep{wang2024wise}, GRACE \citep{hartvigsen2022grace},
MEMOIR \citep{wang2025memoir}, and WikiBigEdit \citep{thede2025wikibigedit} design editors for
long sequences of factual updates. We do not propose an editor; we characterize the plain
SGD-into-LoRA dynamics that agentic memory designs inherit whenever they skip editing machinery
--- the null model those editors improve on --- and we run a minimal GRACE-style arm inside the
instrument (\S\ref{sec:act2}), where it reproduces our central dissociation.

\textbf{Continual learning and replay selection.} Catastrophic forgetting is classical
\citep{mccloskey1989,kirkpatrick2017}; \citet{davari2022probing} show by probing that
representations often survive when task accuracy collapses --- the CL-side precedent for our
recognition-outlives-recall dissociation, here measured behaviorally, per fact, without trained
probes. Replay selection by interference (MIR;
\citealt{aljundi2019mir}), surprise (SuRe; \citealt{sure2025}), forgetting curves (MSSR;
\citealt{mssr2026}), and prioritized replay \citep{schaul2015} all improve on uniform replay; our
miss-gated rule is a member of this family, applied via generative self-testing, and we claim
only its budget-matched interaction with the substrate, its cadence dependence, and its
form-alignment dependence. Orthogonal projection \citep{farajtabar2020ogd} and pseudo-rehearsal
\citep{robins1995} bound the design space.

\textbf{Forgetting as suppression --- and its limits.} In machine unlearning, ``forgotten''
knowledge is frequently recoverable by quantization, relearning, or prompting
\citep{quantization-unlearning,unlearning-deletion,kotha2023}; forgetting-and-relearning events
pervade offline training \citep{toneva2019}. We import the storage/expression distinction
\citep{allenzhu2024} into the online regime with a per-fact recognition meter --- and then bound
what the meter licenses: our savings probe shows 2-AFC discriminability does not imply
accelerated re-acquisition, a distinction the reversibility literature usually elides.

\textbf{Sleep-cycle agents and complementary learning systems.} Architectures that consolidate
daytime experience into parameters at rest are numerous
\citep{lee2026offline,behrouz2026sleep,scm2026,wang2026scol,hoy2025stable,behrouz2024titans,attt2026};
vbario's \texttt{sleeping-llm} \citep{bario2026sleepingllm} is the operationally closest prior
system, released as software rather than as a controlled comparison. These works build and
assert; we instrument and measure a running loop per fact, per night, per age --- and several of
their implicit assumptions (that self-distillation suffices; that serving with all adapters
mounted is free) fail measurably here. The day/core split follows CLS \citep{mcclelland1995};
our results give the computational version two sharp caveats (consolidation must be driven by
ground truth; the fast store interferes at \emph{read} time). The recitation-poisoning dynamics
echo training-on-own-outputs degradation \citep{shumailov2024} inside a single system; the
tension with self-synthesized rehearsal, which \emph{works} for skills \citep{huang2024ssr}, is
taken up in \S\ref{sec:discussion}. Test-time adaptation for \emph{immediate control}
\citep{wang2026adajepa} is the complement of our setting (adaptation for \emph{retention}, where
nothing re-supplies a fact unless it was logged); behavioral horizon scaling
\citep{edgebench2026} measures the behavioral layer above the substrate-level horizon question
that Act 3 measures. RoboTTT \citep{robottt2026} is the sharpest precedent for \emph{day-only}
in-stream weight writing with no sleep phase --- fast weights updated by gradient descent during
inference --- but as weight-space \emph{working} memory at episode scale (context scaling),
distinct from the timeline-scale persistent layer measured here; its meta-learned write rule is
also the literature's fix for exactly the destructive-rewrite artifact that our vanilla-AdamW
writes exhibit (\S\ref{sec:discussion}). One taxonomy pin: weights-frozen memory systems contain
no component that lived through the events they store, so they structurally cannot adjudicate
conflicting records or calibrate to write history (\S\ref{sec:adjudication}); in
continual-learning terms our setting is entry-level online continual learning --- fact-level
index learning, not skill learning.

\textbf{Familiarity, membership, and self-knowledge gates (Act 4's neighbors).} Four adjacent
lines exist; none occupies Act 4's cell. (i) \emph{Membership inference}: adapter-vs-base score
deviation is used to \emph{attack} fine-tuned models --- LoRA-Leak \citep{loraleak2025} against
LoRA adapters, FSD \citep{fsd2024} as the closest mechanical precedent (fine-tune-vs-base
deviation for pretraining-data membership). These validate the signal; we invert the attack into
an organ the system itself consults; and \citet{ferrando2025entity} find causal
entity-recognition directions governing knowledge-awareness in pretrained models --- the
in-representation counterpart of our behavioral familiarity scalar, for pretraining knowledge
rather than online writes. (ii) \emph{Adaptive-RAG gates}: SKR \citep{skr2023}, SeaKR
\citep{seakr2024}, and FLARE \citep{flare2023} gate retrieval on whether the model ``knows this
in general,'' descending from P(IK) calibration \citep{kadavath2022}; our gate asks a different
question --- ``is this in \emph{my} experience?'' --- read from a dedicated memory adapter
against its own frozen base. (iii) \emph{Gated parametric memory}: MEGa \citep{mega2025} gates
LoRA memory to store facts \emph{in} weights --- our converse; RF-Mem \citep{rfmem2026} is the
closest prior overall, using familiarity to adapt retrieval, but its familiarity is
embedding-based and queries the \emph{store}, and it gates retrieval \emph{depth}; ours reads
the \emph{weights} and gates \emph{trust-vs-verify}. (iv) \emph{Calibration objections}: cheap
uncertainty baselines \citep{cheapuncertainty2025} and the finding that internal signals fire on
confidently-wrong generations \citep{confidentwrong2025} are both engaged head-on in
\S\ref{sec:act4} --- the first is answered by a base-only chance row that holds by construction,
the second is \emph{instantiated} by our own self-argmax negative, which we report as such.

\section{The instrument}\label{sec:instrument}

\subsection{Day scale: single-pass fact streams}\label{sec:dayinstrument}

\paragraph{Substrates.} Primary: \qwen{} (24 layers). Transfer: \smol{} \citep{allal2025smollm2}
--- a different organization, architecture family, and training corpus --- run with \emph{all
hyperparameters unchanged}, so transfer tests qualitative structure, not tuned performance. Both
fp32, frozen; one LoRA adapter \citep{hu2022lora} (rank 64, $\alpha{=}128$, all linear modules
unless stated) is the sole trainable memory. AdamW, lr $3\times10^{-5}$, gradient clip 1.0.
Consumer ROCm GPUs; runs are nondeterministic even at fixed seed, so every number is reported
per-seed (seeds 1234/2025/777, plus 7/42 where $n{=}5$) and interpreted as a direction.

\paragraph{Stream.} $N{=}48$ facts of the form \emph{``The user's \{attribute\} is \{value\}.''}
with unique attributes and unique three-syllable pseudoword values (collision-free; unguessable;
answers cannot be produced without the write). One fact per turn, single pass: 8 gradient steps
on the fact (loss on value tokens only), then the mechanism's bookkeeping, then (for replay
mechanisms) exactly $m{=}4$ replay gradient steps on stored past facts. Budgets are identical
across mechanisms. A second variant draws 48 real-entity counterfactual edits from CounterFact
\citep{meng2022rome} with unique subjects and targets, adding real tokens and dataset paraphrase
prompts (Appendix~\ref{app:cf}).

\paragraph{Mechanisms.} \emph{naked}: writes only. \emph{BF}: Benna--Fusi multi-timescale
synaptic cascade \citep{benna2016} on adapter parameters (details and sweep:
Appendix~\ref{app:bf}). \emph{EWC}: elastic weight consolidation \citep{kirkpatrick2017} in the
accumulating regime --- diagonal Fisher of each fact \emph{summed} into a running Fisher (never
rebuilt), anchor re-set after each fact, $\lambda{=}300$ (sweeping $\lambda \in \{100, 300,
1000\}$ on the primary substrate gives mean final recall 9.0/10.7/6.5 --- no freeze at any
$\lambda$). \emph{replay}: 4 steps/turn on buffer samples, uniform.
\emph{ewc+replay}: both. \emph{Selection policy}: uniform, or \emph{miss} --- replay targets
drawn first from facts that failed the most recent self-test, filled uniformly. The default
self-test is the recall probe itself (a measurement-independent variant is the control in
\S\ref{sec:act3day}); the information it uses --- which stored facts currently fail --- is
available to any deployed agent that keeps a log of what it wrote. Self-test cadence defaults to
one test per 2 writes (swept in \S\ref{sec:act3day}, full table in Appendix~\ref{app:cadence}).

\paragraph{Probes.} Every 2 writes, all facts so far are probed. \emph{Recall}: greedy completion
of the cloze prefix; substring match on the value. \emph{Recognition} (the 2-AFC discrimination tier): sum log-probability margin
of the true value vs.\ a fixed distractor value sampled from the \emph{same stream} --- the
distractor was written into the same adapter with the same procedure, so a positive margin
measures fact-specific binding, not token familiarity. Chance is 24/48. The distractor draw is
deterministic (seeded per fact, fixed in code before any run), and the meter's zero point is
verified directly: on the \emph{untrained} base models the identical comparison --- same pairs,
same draw --- scores 20--25/48 (Qwen) and 18--27/48 (SmolLM2) across seeds, i.e.\ at chance, with
gold and distractor token counts balanced ($\approx$6.0--6.2 vs.\ 5.9--6.7) and a per-token
length-normalized variant giving the same picture. \emph{Recovery} (pinned
definition): a fact is a recovery candidate if it was recalled at some probe and missed at a
later one; it recovered if it is recalled again after its first miss. \emph{Firewall}: GSM8K
(10-item fixed subset) with the adapter disabled, before and after the stream
(\S\ref{sec:firewall}).

\paragraph{Tier terminology.} Three readout tiers recur throughout, ordered by how much the
probe supplies; we fix the names here.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
tier & probe & canonical name \\
\midrule
emission & free recall: greedy generation of the value & recall \\
key & gold token in the top-$k$ at the answer slot & key tier \\
discrimination & 2-AFC margin over two presented alternatives & discrimination \\
\bottomrule

``Recognition'' remains the informal umbrella for the non-emission tiers where it reads
naturally. \emph{Authentication} is reserved for exactly one primitive --- the candidate-free

\subsection{The loop: days, nights, and a month of consolidation}\label{sec:loopinstrument}

Frozen \qwen{} base (fp32, eager attention); persistent rank-32 \textsc{core} adapter +
disposable rank-64 \textsc{day} adapter (both all-linear LoRA, $\alpha{=}2r$; AdamW, lr
$3{\times}10^{-5}$); 24 facts per day from the day-scale generator. \textbf{Day}: each fact gets
8 value-masked write steps into \textsc{day} with summed-Fisher EWC ($\lambda{=}300$) plus 4
replay steps on the most recent self-test's failures (the day-scale winner,
\S\ref{sec:act3day}); serving state \textsc{core+day}. \textbf{Night}: consolidate into
\textsc{core}, then delete and re-initialize \textsc{day}. The gradient budget is fixed at $72 =
24\times3$ steps per night for every arm except \emph{full}: \emph{full} = all past true
statements, 3 epochs (budget grows with history; the reference ceiling, deliberately \textbf{not}
budget-matched); \emph{markov} = today's true statements only (the scalable recency baseline);
\emph{misslog} = probe \textsc{core}-alone recall over the whole history (a generation cost that
grows linearly with history, not a gradient cost), then train only on current failures, from the
log; \emph{recite} = self-distill the core's own greedy completions of the whole history, no log;
\emph{hybrid} = today from the log + old facts self-recited, budget spread uniformly.

\paragraph{Probes and fact classes.} After every night (every third night at the 30-day horizon),
\textsc{core}-only greedy recall and 2-AFC recognition margins over \emph{all} facts so far. At
each probe every fact is assigned one of three classes: \textbf{recalled} --- greedy recall
produces the true value; \textbf{latent} --- not recalled, but the 2-AFC margin is positive
(recognized-but-not-recalled; per \S\ref{sec:savings} this label asserts discriminability only);
\textbf{gone} --- neither. Next-day survival of a class is the fraction of its members recalled
at the following probe. Dual-state capability probes (GSM8K and fixed-text NLL/token in both
serving states) are in \S\ref{sec:firewall}. Seeds 1234/2025/777 (D6); 1234/2025 at D30 for all
three horizon arms. Fact cohorts at equal seed are prefix-identical across horizons, so
D6/D12/D30 comparisons are exact.

Table~\ref{tab:main} spans a $12\times$ range in final retention (3.7 to 44.2 of 48). If
mechanisms differed by making facts \emph{persist} longer, time-to-first-miss would covary with
that range. It does not: on the primary substrate the median fact first fails 3--8 writes after
acquisition under every mechanism we compose (the window widens elsewhere: 4--18 on \smol{}). Under naked SGD the store is, in effect, a $\sim$1.5-batch
FIFO holding the last handful of writes. What covaries with retention is what happens \emph{after} the first
miss: under naked writes 4\% of missed facts ever return; under uniform replay 76\%; under the
error-gated composition 94\%. End-of-stream alive counts tell the same story: naked 11/144, EWC
32/144, replay 57/144, composition 184/240, error-gated composition 221/240.

\emph{Persistence is not accumulation.} Online in-weight memory in this regime is not a leaky
tank whose drain rate mechanisms adjust; it is a \emph{flickering} store in which nearly
everything falls below the recall threshold quickly, and capacity is determined by the
re-instatement process. Aggregate accuracy and half-life statistics cannot see this structure;
per-fact timelines can. The same dynamic is known \emph{inside} offline training as example
forgetting events \citep{toneva2019}: shuffled multi-epoch training is, in effect, a built-in
re-instatement schedule. The online regime obeys the same law, with the schedule made explicit
and paid for.

\subsection{Expression is form-bound: the paraphrase collapse}\label{sec:paraphrase}

Replacing the synthetic stream with 48 real-entity counterfactual edits from CounterFact
\citep{meng2022rome} preserves every ordering --- the mechanism ladder, the recovery gradient
(34\% $\to$ 87\%), full recognition behind the compositions, and the firewall
($0.70\to0.70$; full table and per-arm numbers in Appendix~\ref{app:cf}) --- and adds the
decisive measurement: across the five mechanism arms, paraphrase recall spans
\textbf{12--31\%}; the composition adapters that recall $\sim$90--95\% in the write form and
recognize $\sim$100\% answer only \textbf{21--25\%} of paraphrase prompts (per-seed
19--33\%). The three probes
expose a readout hierarchy --- \textbf{recognition $\gg$ write-form recall $\gg$ paraphrase
recall} --- which turns the usual form-matching caveat into a measured quantity. What online
writes create is overwhelmingly \emph{form-bound} binding; generalized expression is the
scarcest resource. A store you can only read back in the exact phrasing you wrote is not,
functionally, a store.

\subsection{What forgetting leaves behind --- and the savings probe that bounds it}
\label{sec:savings}

The recognition column of Table~\ref{tab:main} dissociates two kinds of forgetting. Under naked
writes, recognition of the full stream is at or modestly above chance (23--33 vs.\ chance 24 on
\qwen{}; 29--30 on \smol{}) --- one \qwen{} seed reaches 33/48, which is $p\approx0.007$ under
the binomial null, so ``erasure'' here means that \emph{most}, not all, fact-specific binding is
destroyed when recall of a fact dies. Under accumulated EWC, recall is poor but recognition is
far above chance (38--45 of 48 on \qwen{} at 10.7 recalled). Whether forgetting is erasure or a
residual trace \emph{depends on what protected the weights while subsequent writes arrived}:
unprotected interference destroys discriminability; Fisher-anchored interference preserves it.

In earlier versions we described the protected regime as ``suppression --- the weights still
hold what the readout lost.'' A savings probe we ran on our own strongest case forces a weaker
and more precise statement. On the purest arm (EWC on \smol{}, 3 seeds; recall 0/0/0,
recognition 31/36/38), we reset to the end-of-stream adapter per fact,
took $k\in\{1,2\}$ plain gradient steps on that fact alone, and compared the 48 written (OLD)
facts against 48 same-distribution never-written (NOVEL) facts. If a recoverable latent trace
existed, old facts should relearn faster. \textbf{They do not}: the $\Delta$log-prob gain is
statistically indistinguishable between OLD and NOVEL in every seed at both $k$ (e.g., old
$+5.35$ vs.\ novel $+5.88$; old $+13.65$ vs.\ novel $+12.66$), with the sign flipping across
seeds; recall stays 0--1/48 in both arms at $k\le2$ (the single exception is one old-fact
recall at $k{=}2$ on seed 777; a floor effect either way: the originals were written
with 8 steps), and a zero-step sanity check gives 0/48.

All ``latent'' language in this paper is therefore the bounded claim: \emph{a residual trace
that supports 2-AFC discrimination but neither expression nor accelerated re-acquisition}. The
hostile reading ($\lambda{=}300$ mis-scaled on \smol{}, leaving the adapter nearly frozen) is
\emph{not} excluded by this probe; we say so plainly. Caveats: $k\le2$ only; one substrate;
3 seeds. Act 1's verdict: as a \emph{store} --- something that persists, generalizes across
forms, and can be re-opened cheaply --- the online adapter fails on all three counts.

%=====================================================================
\section{Act 2: Recognition survives --- cross-mechanism}\label{sec:act2}
%=====================================================================

\subsection{The latent class is the dominant survivor}\label{sec:latent}

Behind every protected write rule, what remains when recall dies is recognition. On \qwen{},
EWC recalls 10.7 while recognizing 38--45 of 48; replay-based mechanisms recognize 40--48 while
recalling 16--46; the error-gated composition recognizes \emph{everything} (48$\times$5) while
recalling 41--46 (Table~\ref{tab:main}). The purest case is the substrate transfer: on \smol{},
with every hyperparameter carried over unchanged, EWC recalls \textbf{0/48 while recognizing
31--38/48} --- everything the readout lost remains 2-AFC-discriminable behind the failing
generative readout (full transfer tables in Appendix~\ref{app:transfer}; the structure
replicates, the magnitudes do not, and regularization strengths tuned on one substrate can
silently zero the readout on another).

Depth tells the same story in space. At matched adapter capacity, \emph{recall} concentrates in
the last third of the stack --- opposite to the early-layer placement reported for batch
injection \citep{back2026lora} --- while recognition shows the middle third \emph{stores} nearly
as much as the full stack (42.7 vs.\ 43.3 of 48 recognized under replay) and expresses a quarter
of it: the depth gradient of online writes is an \emph{expression} gradient, not a storage
gradient (full band tables and discussion in Appendix~\ref{app:depth}).

\subsection{A different mechanism, the same phenotype: the GRACE-style editor}\label{sec:grace}

Is recognition-without-recall an artifact of SGD? We implemented a minimal GRACE-style discrete
key--value adaptor \citep{hartvigsen2022grace} inside the instrument (one wrapped Linear at
$\approx$0.75 depth; keys = input activation at the last cloze token; learned output offsets
within a fixed deferral radius; pre-registered 2026-07-13, all four predictions held; full
setup, budgets, and CounterFact numbers in Appendix~\ref{app:grace}). Two headlines. At a
canonical budget ($\sim$12$\times$ the SGD write compute), the editor reaches \emph{parity} with
the best SGD arm (recall 43.3 vs.\ 44.2), not superiority --- its losses trace to $\varepsilon$-ball
key collisions, a qualitatively different failure mode from weight interference. At a
\emph{matched} budget (8 steps per fact), it recalls \textbf{1/0/0 against recognition
47/45/47} --- an extreme recognition-without-recall dissociation from a mechanism that shares no
machinery with SGD-into-LoRA.

\textbf{The latent phenotype is cross-mechanism}: recognition-without-recall appears whenever
the write is under-powered relative to the expression threshold --- EWC-protected SGD, a
key--value editor, restricted depth bands, weak substrates. Recognition is the last readout to
die. Act 2's verdict: what the online adapter reliably \emph{keeps} is not the ability to
produce its contents but the ability to \emph{recognize} them --- the defining property of an
index, not a store.

%=====================================================================
\section{Act 3: Truth must live in the log}\label{sec:act3}
%=====================================================================

If the weights hold a recognition index and expression decays, then expressed truth has to come
from somewhere that does not decay: a ground-truth log. Act 3 shows this twice over --- the log
is what makes cheap re-instatement possible (\S\ref{sec:act3day}, \S\ref{sec:policies}), and its
absence is not neutral but actively toxic (\S\ref{sec:nolog}).

A scope note before the arms. Every night arm below is measured on the two-adapter day/core
split of \S\ref{sec:loopinstrument}, with recall-tuned 8-step writes. Under the write policy the
ladder later selects --- single-step writes plus miss-gated replay, one adapter
(\S\ref{sec:ladder}) --- the picture changes: the day loop is itself continuous error-gated
micro-consolidation, and a separate night phase becomes optional rather than structural (the
marathon of \S\ref{sec:marathon} runs without one). Act 3's laws are therefore about what
nights must look like \emph{when they are run}.

\subsection{Day scale: error-gated re-instatement from the log, at unchanged gradient budget}
\label{sec:act3day}

Holding the replay budget fixed at 4 steps/turn and changing only \emph{selection} --- replay
facts that failed the agent's most recent self-test first --- helps every replay-bearing arm on
both substrates: on \qwen{}, plain replay moves $19.0\to23.3$ as a direction only (the seed
triples overlap: 24/16/17 uniform vs.\ 15/28/27 miss --- we say this plainly), while the
composition moves $36.8\to\textbf{44.2}$ with barely overlapping distributions (miss minimum 41
vs.\ uniform maximum 42; $n{=}5$ each); on \smol{}, replay $26.0\to32.3$ and composition
$22.7\to36.0$ (gains +4.3/+7.4/+6.3/+13.3; compositions helped most). Recovery-after-miss rises
to 87--94\%. We do not claim the selection rule as novel
\citep{aljundi2019mir,sure2025,mssr2026}; the contributions are the budget-matched
decomposition, the cadence dose--response, and the circularity control below. ``Unchanged
budget'' refers to \emph{gradient} budget: the self-test costs generation-time compute, priced
explicitly in the loop (\S\ref{sec:loopinstrument}).

Two qualifiers are load-bearing and we state them wherever the 44.2 appears. \emph{Freshness}:
the gain decays monotonically with self-test cadence (44.2 $\to$ 35.3 $\to$ 32.0 at one test per
2/6/12 writes; recovery 94\% $\to$ 75\% $\to$ 53\%; Table~\ref{tab:cadence},
Appendix~\ref{app:cadence}) ---
stale miss lists degrade targeting back to uniformity. \emph{Form alignment}: two independent
audits noted that the default self-test \emph{is} the evaluation probe, so we ran the control ---
gating on an \emph{interrogative} self-test while evaluation stays the declarative cloze yields
\textbf{40/44/33, mean 39.0} vs.\ 44.2 form-matched and 36.8 uniform: roughly 5/48 of the
headline gain is coupled to self-test/readout form alignment, which we concede; the margin of
the form-independent gate over uniform ($+2.2$) is a direction only at $n{=}3$. The mechanism of
the drop is itself on-thesis: the question-form self-test passes only \textbf{1--6/48} of facts
the cloze readout can express (probe-vs-self-test agreement 6--14/48) --- the same
form-generalization gap as the paraphrase collapse (\S\ref{sec:paraphrase}), measured on the
readout that Act 4 will show still carries near-perfect \emph{familiarity}. With nearly every
fact flagged as a miss, the gate floods toward uniform-over-misses; most of the drop is
self-test \emph{insensitivity}, not gain fakeness.

In cognitive terms the composition implements Leitner-style spaced repetition with retrieval
practice \citep{roediger2006} in weight space; the freshness requirement is the part the
flash-card analogy does predict.

\subsection{Nights without a log: recitation does not just fail, it poisons}\label{sec:nolog}

Pure self-recitation collapses structurally (0/144, recognition at chance): in a day/core split,
the core cannot recite content that only the day adapter learned, so the night trains on noise.
This is an architectural fact and one half of why the log is load-bearing. The \emph{hybrid} arm
was pre-registered (2026-07-08) to rescue it: today's facts from the log, old facts recited,
same budget --- our prediction placed it between markov and misslog. \textbf{The prediction was
falsified downward: 4.3/144, below markov.} Per-cohort traces show why: hybrid's today-cohort
consolidates normally at its own night (7/9/4 of 24, comparable to markov) and is then
\emph{erased} over subsequent nights, reaching $\sim$0 by day 6, while markov's cohorts merely
decay passively (own-night cohort retention 4--20 of 24, falling to 4--7 of 24 one night
later). Hybrid \emph{actively rehearses wrong values}
--- fluently, plausibly, in exactly the training format --- and gradient descent cannot tell
fluent errors from truth: a within-system analogue of model collapse \citep{shumailov2024}, and
the second half of why the log is load-bearing. \textbf{If ground truth is unavailable for the
past, doing nothing about the past (markov) beats self-distilling it (hybrid) by $6\times$.} On
\smol{} the poisoning is harsher still (hybrid 0.5/144; Appendix~\ref{app:transfer}).

\subsection{The capability firewall, and what serving costs}\label{sec:firewall}

\paragraph{Firewall (adapter-off).} Disabling the adapter returns exactly the base model's GSM8K
score ($0.70\to0.70$ on \qwen{}; $0.40\to0.40$ on \smol{}; fixed 10-item subset) ---
\textbf{verified on all 39 composition runs (both substrates)}; for the remaining arms it
\textbf{holds by construction for adapter-off serving} (the base is frozen; adapter-off is
bit-identical to base). An earlier version said ``checked in every run''; a cold audit of the
released artifacts found firewall data in the composition files only, and we correct the
wording here. Online writing at any tested intensity costs the frozen base nothing when the
adapter is unmounted.

\paragraph{Mounted capability.} At 10-item resolution, GSM8K is 0.70--1.00 with the core alone
and 0.70--0.90 with the hot day adapter mounted, vs.\ base 0.70 --- 24/24 measurements at or
above base. A 100-item frozen GSM8K subset gives $10\times$ resolution: \qwen{} base 0.64; the
D6 error-gated cores score 0.66 and 0.60 --- $|\Delta|\le0.04$, within binomial noise
($\pm{\sim}0.05$). (\smol{} base reference: 0.37.) The day adapters of those runs were not
saved, so \textbf{``not aphasic'' is scoped to core-only serving}; the hot \textsc{core+day}
evidence is NLL plus the 10-item reading.

\paragraph{Fluency accent and read-time masking.} Free-text NLL/token rises from 2.93 (base) to
3.21--3.58 with the core alone --- growing slowly with consolidated days --- and spans 3.1--5.0
under the hot day mount, reaching $\sim$6$\times$ perplexity at its worst. The tax is
super-additive at the high end (each adapter alone is mild) but not uniform: the two day-0
measurements sit below the same-day core-alone value. The same hot state damages \emph{memory} readout: at the 30-day
final night the error-gated run recalls 70/720 with the day adapter attached and 326/720 without
it; even full replay shows 678 vs.\ 713. A freshly-written day adapter dominates the readout and
emits its own day's values for old prompts: \textbf{concurrent adapter serving taxes old-fact
recall} --- final-probe ratios $4.7\times$ and $15\times$ on the error-gated arm ($2.3$--$41.5\times$
across all its D30 probes), $1.8$--$2.3\times$ for recency-only, and $1.05\times$ for full
replay. Both the $6\times$ accent and the masking are properties of the \emph{two-adapter}
\textsc{core+day} mount on the 2B loop substrate. On the 9B marathon substrate --- a single
maintained adapter, hot after $\sim$1920 optimizer steps (seed 1234) --- GSM8K-100 goes 0.79
base $\to$ 0.80 adapter-on (structured reasoning unharmed) and neutral-text NLL/token
$2.393\to2.927$ ($+0.53$, perplexity $1.7\times$): an order milder than the two-adapter 2B hot
state's $6\times$. The deployment prescription is accordingly not ``serve from the slow store''
but: \textbf{do not serve two adapters concurrently}; a single maintained adapter shows neither
the masking nor the aphasia; and for long free-text generation, extract-with-adapter $\to$
generate-with-base removes the accent at zero cost. The mild accent itself deserves a reframe:
it is a signature of experience, not damage --- a component that lived through $\sim$2000
updates \emph{should} bear stylistic traces, and the frozen base is clean precisely because it
has no history. The honest boundary: accent is a spectrum with a pathological end. The 2B
two-adapter $6\times$ came \emph{with} degeneration; the $1.7\times$ comes with intact reasoning
and protocol compliance --- two points, with no dose--response curve measured between them ---
and long-form serving is where even the mild accent bills (\S\ref{sec:inmind}).

Act 3's verdict: the weights cannot be the system's source of truth --- but a system whose truth
lives in a log needs something the weights turn out to be uniquely positioned to provide.

%=====================================================================
\section{Act 4: The surviving recognition is the key to using the log}\label{sec:act4}
%=====================================================================

A log-backed agent faces a question retrieval similarity cannot answer: when content is put in
front of it --- a retrieved log entry, an upstream model's draft, a user's claim --- \emph{did I
experience this?} Acts 1--3 established that the adapter's recognition channel outlives every
other readout. Act 4 tests whether that channel can be read \emph{without candidates} --- as a
familiarity signal over presented content --- and whether it can gate anything. (Familiarity
vs.\ recollection is the classic dual-process distinction in human memory; our latent-class data
are its cross-mechanism machine analogue.)

\subsection{The familiarity signal: candidate-free \texorpdfstring{$\dlp$}{dlp} at presentation}
\label{sec:famsignal}

For a fact $f$ presented in form $\phi$, define
\[
\dlp(f,\phi) \;=\; \log p_{\mathrm{adapter}}\bigl(\mathrm{content}\mid\mathrm{prompt}(\phi)\bigr)
\;-\; \log p_{\mathrm{base}}\bigl(\mathrm{content}\mid\mathrm{prompt}(\phi)\bigr)
\]
--- the adapter-vs-base log-probability of the \emph{presented} content, requiring no generation
and no candidate set.
We score 48 written facts against 48 never-written same-distribution facts --- the harder and
correct null for ``did I experience this'': same templates, same pseudoword lexicon, answer- and
attribute-collision-filtered, so nothing but write history separates the two sets,
per saved adapter.

\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4.5pt}
\begin{tabular}{llccc}
\toprule

(31--38/48): the signal is graded in the strength of the underlying memory, as an index should
be.

\subsection{A minimal gate: what worked, what failed, and why the failure is informative}
\label{sec:gate}

Before running, we specified three predictions in the working log (2026-07-15; unlike the other
pre-registrations in this paper, these were not committed to the dated ledger file before
execution --- we flag the distinction rather than blur it). The demo: 96 queries per run
(48 written + 48 never-written attributes; the log contains only the written facts), with three
predictions --- (a) ungated, the mounted model fabricates fluent pseudoword values for
never-written attributes at a near-total rate; (b) a gate on the generated candidate's $\dlp$
suppresses fabrication; (c) a threshold calibrated on one seed transfers frozen to the others.
\textbf{Prediction (a) held; (b) and (c) were missed.} We report all three, together with a
form asymmetry the runs surfaced: the gate that failed was run in the question form; the gate
that works is a cloze-form (write-form) instrument.

\emph{Ungated fabrication is $\approx$100\%}: on never-written attributes the adapter-mounted
model produces a confident value-like completion essentially every time --- the write template
generalizes as a \emph{template} (template pull) even where no binding exists. This is the
failure mode a memory-writing agent actually ships with.

\emph{Candidate-scored gates fail, for a reason worth having on record.} Across four timeboxed
gate iterations, every gate that scored the model's \emph{own generated answer} by $\dlp$ ---
these are the question-form runs --- failed: the adapter's own greedy output always sits at the
peak of its own distribution, so the
candidate's $\dlp$ measures the adapter's \emph{sharpening} of its output distribution, not
storage --- a self-argmax bias. A threshold frozen on the calibration seed does \emph{not}
transfer: \textbf{97.9\% / 95.8\% of fabrications pass through} on the held-out seeds. We report
this as a clean negative that \emph{instantiates}, in a controlled setting, the warning that
internal confidence signals fire on confidently-wrong generations \citep{confidentwrong2025}:
scoring your own argmax is exactly how a system becomes confidently wrong.

\emph{An answer-independent signal partially works --- in the write form.} Slot-KL --- the
divergence
$\mathrm{KL}(\mathrm{adapter}\,\|\,\mathrm{base})$ between the next-token distributions at the
answer slot, computed before any generation --- asks not ``do I believe my answer?'' but ``does
my memory reshape this slot at all?''. Run in the \emph{cloze} form the facts were written in,
with a threshold calibrated on one seed and then
\textbf{frozen},
it cuts fabrication from $\approx$100\% to \textbf{25.0\% / 33.3\% / 10.4\%} across the three
seeds, at a written-side coverage of \textbf{52--56\%} (fraction of written queries the gate
routes to the log or trusts correctly). A partial gate, honestly reported: it transfers, it
leaves half the written side on the floor, and it is in-form (cloze) only --- the question-form
attempt is the candidate-scored failure above. Slot-KL is, in the tally of
\S\ref{sec:readoutgap}, the last surviving fire/no-fire instrument, and only in the write form.

\subsection{The readout gap, and what the organ is for}\label{sec:readoutgap}

Put the two halves of Act 4 side by side. When content is \emph{presented}, the adapter
recognizes its own experience at AUC \textbf{0.93--0.99}. When the system must read the same
signal out \emph{unprompted} --- from its own candidates or from a pre-generation slot statistic
--- the best separation we achieved is Youden $J = 0.27$ on the calibration seed; on the two
held-out seeds the same threshold family reaches $J = 0.35$ and $0.50$, so the bound is a
calibration-seed statement, not a ceiling. \textbf{Recognized when shown; hard
to read out unprompted.} This mirrors the human recognition/recall asymmetry --- reported
for few-shot fine-tuned LLM memories by \citet{orhan2023recognition}, whose
exposure-ordering and interference-robustness findings the stress test's dose wall
replicates on a controlled axis --- now measured in
one substrate with matched probes, and it joins the recall law of \S\ref{sec:openproblem} as
this paper's closing open problem.

The asymmetry is not an artifact of our first two instruments. A third, stronger attempt --- a
presented-mode binding statistic computed over the full log-value inventory, with per-value
leave-one-out centering, so no generation artifact can enter --- still fails as a fire/no-fire
signal: AUC \textbf{0.63} on direct queries and \textbf{0.38} on referential ones, at the level
of the earlier instruments. Three independent instruments failing identically is substrate
physics, not implementation error. But the \emph{same statistic used as a ranking} works:
pairwise AUC 0.883, gold at median rank 3 over the inventory, recovering 47.8\% of first-pass
retrieval misses into the top-4; unioned with lexical retrieval it lifts hit rate
$0.521\to0.750$ --- a written-vs-unwritten contrast; on an all-written log the same scan ranks
at chance (\S\ref{sec:inmind}): familiarity ranks provenance, not relevance --- and register
spillover from unrelated writes can defeat even the provenance contrast at low dose, burying
a lightly-written line beneath unwritten lines that merely resemble the stream. The channel
supports finding-when-looking far better than knowing-you-have-it.

The fourth instrument is the model's own thinking, and it returns the sharpest form of the
negative: \emph{honest nescience}. In a tool-using agent with a forced first line ---
``EXPECT: YES/NO'' before any tool result --- the adapter-carrying model answers NO on all 48
written and all 24 unwritten queries (separation exactly zero), verbalizing ``I don't have any
specific impression\ldots''; the scan then ranks the gold line into the top-4 and it answers
correctly. No confabulated familiarity --- but no channel to real familiarity either. A
$2\times2$ prompt-attribution matrix settles where the self-report comes from: an
active-framing prompt yields NO/NO (adapter/base) and a passive-framing prompt yields YES/YES
(147/148) --- self-report about memory is an echo of the prompt's framing, with zero memory
contribution in either polarity (observational; single prompt per arm). The deployment
corollary is blunt: never build UX or control flow on asking the model ``do you know $X$?''.

It also settles what the organ is for. The 0.93--0.99 regime is available exactly when the
content is \emph{externally fixed} --- like the gold answers in our probe. So familiarity should
score \emph{external} content: retrieved log entries, an upstream model's drafts, a user's
claims. Wrong external claims do not false-fire: the same adapters' 2-AFC margins prefer the
true value over an equally-trained wrong value in \textbf{48/48} cases --- a
\emph{within-attribute} statement (both candidates answer the same attribute); the
cross-attribute binding hole is measured at the end of this subsection. What familiarity must
\emph{never} score is the memory adapter's own generations --- that is the self-argmax trap. The
division of labor that Acts 1--4 converge on: \textbf{generation upstream (base or a
stronger model), the log as the truth store, the adapter as a recognition organ over external
content}. We call the resulting primitive \emph{experience authentication}: retrieval similarity
answers ``does this match semantically''; familiarity answers ``did \emph{I} experience this''
--- a question no store-side signal can answer, because it is a property of the reader, not the
content. System-grade evaluation --- behavioral compliance with familiarity annotations, real
workloads, public hallucination benchmarks --- is explicitly future work; our on-distribution
fabrication measure is deliberately narrow (pseudoword construction guarantees the model
\emph{cannot} know the answer, so fabrication is unconfounded with pretraining knowledge).

One hole in the authentication story is measured and open: \textbf{content vs.\ binding}. Under
opaque referential questions with a mismatched retrieved context, fabrication on unwritten
queries rises $0.08\to\textbf{0.71}$ in a forced-context probe, and reaches \textbf{0.235} in
the agent loop of \S\ref{sec:e2edemo} (vs.\ 0.000 on direct forms); in all four agent cases the
model answered a \emph{genuinely written} value to the wrong question, and 3 of the 4 passed
the $\dlp$ authentication signal. A post-hoc binding gate built from the same presented-mode statistic
cannot close the hole (AUC 0.43, below chance --- it re-approves its own ranking). $\dlp$
authenticates \emph{content}, not \emph{binding}; the fix requires an independent,
relevance-verifiable channel. Every fabrication figure in this paper therefore carries its form
scope: the near-zero rates are direct-form results.

\subsection{A mechanism for the readout gap: present in the workspace, lost in emission}\label{sec:jspace}

Why does recognition outlive recall? A recent interpretability result gives us a vocabulary and a
probe. \citet{jspace2026} identify a small ``verbalizable'' subspace --- a functional global
workspace, read out per token by a Jacobian lens (J-lens) --- that carries the content a model can
report and reason with, atop a much larger volume of automatic processing. We adapt the J-lens to
our answer slot: on the \emph{frozen base}, we compute the direction whose amplitude at the cloze
position drives each fact's gold answer token, so the measuring stick is fixed while the adapter's
memory state varies. Across the 48-fact day-scale streams we then read each fact's J-projection
at every probe,
grouped by its recall/recognition state, under two mechanisms that forget at very different depths:
ewcreplay (shallow: $43/48$ end-of-stream recall) and naked SGD (deep: $1\text{--}2/48$ recall,
$22\text{--}28/48$ still recognized), three seeds each (Fig.~\ref{fig:jspace}).

The workspace amplitude \emph{co-tracks the readout hierarchy}, and the deep-forgetting mechanism
is what makes the claim falsifiable rather than vacuous. Recalled facts project high under both
mechanisms ($0.222$ / $0.218$). Recognized-but-not-recalled facts --- \emph{the readout gap itself}
--- sit clearly above the null floor under both ($0.175$ / $0.110$ vs.\ a mismatch-token null at
$\approx0.08$ and a matched-norm random null at $0$; the nulls are direct descendants of the
dimension-dependence caveats that topological data analysis taught us). But facts that are \emph{not even recognized}
collapse to the null: under naked SGD, where this class is well populated ($n\!=\!678$), gone facts
project at $0.090\approx$ null. (The shallow run's higher gone value, $0.137$, rests on only
$n\!=\!11$ and was, we now see, a shallow-forgetting artifact --- a reading our own deeper run
corrects.) So the honest statement is not ``the answer is always present'' but the sharper one the
figure shows: \textbf{workspace amplitude survives exactly as far as recognition does, and no
further.} The readout gap is precisely the band where the content is still in the workspace but has
lost the emission competition; once recognition also fails, the content is genuinely gone --- which,
reassuringly, is where the meter bottoms out. In the shallow regime we can watch the competition
directly: an event-aligned view of each fact's \emph{first miss} shows the projection dipping only
$0.222\!\to\!0.174$ ($-21\%$, still $\gg$ null) and \emph{returning} to $0.223$ on recovery, so a
recalled$\to$missed$\to$recovered fact never leaves the workspace. A companion check confirms the
flip: an unrecalled (but recognized) gold token sits at \emph{median rank 1--2 across seeds}
(small $n$) at the output, not
buried --- the same generable-but-not-emitted knowledge \citet{insideout2025} find in the
pretraining setting, here on an online forgetting timeline. \textbf{Forgetting, in this regime, is rank displacement, not amplitude loss} --- which
is exactly why a light re-instatement (Act 3) rescues it, why recognition (a comparison over
presented content, not an emission) is the last readout to die, and why scoring one's own argmax
(\S\ref{sec:act4}) is circular: the incumbent winner always looks familiar. The readout gap is
still an open problem quantitatively (the recall law of \S\ref{sec:openproblem}), but it now has a
mechanism: the index keeps the content available; the store's job is to win the competition the
weights have stopped winning. We do not claim phenomenal consciousness --- only, following
\citet{jspace2026}, access: the content is available for report while the report itself fails.

The mechanism suggests an obvious remedy, and testing it yields a third confirmation of the
paper's central design rule. If the answer is present at median rank 1--2, let the model
generate its own
top-$k$ candidates and select among them by familiarity. We ran exactly this on the strongest
adapters (three seeds). The candidates do contain the gold answer, but the familiarity selector
($\dlp$) prefers written-syllable recombinations --- ``frankensteins'' assembled from previously
written values, every part of which the adapter scores as familiar --- over the true answer: net
recall \emph{drops} in every seed ($42\!\to\!31$, $43\!\to\!36$, $46\!\to\!39$ of $48$). Full-sequence adapter-likelihood rescoring is safe
(it never regresses, $+1$) but its end-of-stream headroom is small, because by then miss-based
replay has already re-instated everything rescuable and the residue is genuinely gone. The lesson
is the one Act 4 already drew, now shown a third way: \textbf{familiarity authenticates external
content; it cannot select among the reader's own generations} --- scoring your own candidates is
the self-argmax trap wearing a new hat. The fair test of self-candidate rescue is the
\emph{transient} in-stream miss (where J-amplitude is measured high), which we leave to future
work. (Method caveat: the answer-slot J-lens is a deliberate narrowing of the original all-context
Jacobian to the single output token we can label; the co-tracking result is reported across a
shallow and a deep mechanism, but both use the same $2$B substrate and pseudoword facts.)

\section{Act 5: The design, run}\label{sec:act5}
%=====================================================================

Act 4 established what the signal is; Act 5 runs the design. We now treat the index thesis as
an engineering spec and audit it end to end: how forgetting is structured tier by tier (the
ladder), what the write dose should be, how the notebook is read and what that read path costs,
whether the operating windows survive the deployment form and model scale, how far the
maintained regime carries when driven to length (the marathon), where the in-weight channel is
genuinely irreplaceable (adjudication), and whether an agent can drive the whole loop without
being led (the demos). Each subsection is a measured answer; the same floors-and-directions
discipline applies.

\subsection{The readout ladder: forgetting has half-lives, tier by tier}\label{sec:ladder}

The rank-displacement mechanism predicts that ``forgetting'' should not be one event but a
sequence of readout failures of increasing severity. We measured this directly: on the naked
(unmaintained) stream, probing every two writes across three seeds, we track for each fact
(i) free recall, (ii) whether the gold first token remains in the top-$10$ at the answer slot
--- the tier a retrieval-key design depends on --- and (iii) $2$-AFC discrimination. The three
tiers separate cleanly (Figure~\ref{fig:ladder}A): half-lives of $\mathbf{2}$, $\mathbf{7}$,
and $\mathbf{16}$ fact-writes respectively, with a heavy tail --- roughly a third of facts stay
key-alive and discriminable $30$ writes out. A fact that recall has lost keeps its answer in
the top-$10$ for about five more writes (median) and stays discriminable for about fourteen.
Under error-gated replay the key tier holds at $0.94$--$1.0$ indefinitely: maintenance does not
merely slow the ladder, it stops it. The taxonomy here is convergent with recent findings that
catastrophic forgetting is an accessibility failure rather than erasure, measured with trained
probes in supervised vision settings \citep{accessibility2026}; what the ladder adds is that the
levels are \emph{probe-free behavioral readouts} with measurable, dissociable half-lives on an
online fact stream --- and that they prescribe a system's operating windows.

\textbf{Write dose buys acquisition, not capacity.} The per-fact write budget in all experiments
above ($8$ SGD steps) was tuned, originally, for recall. If the adapter is an index, that tuning
is wrong on its face --- and it is. Sweeping the dose over $\{1,2,4,8\}$ steps (three seeds each;
Figure~\ref{fig:ladder}B): acquisition rises with dose ($0.58\!\to\!1.00$ at $2$-AFC), but
\emph{end-state} recognition is flat at $\approx 0.5$ of the stream regardless, and the end-state
key tier is \emph{monotone-decreasing} in dose ($0.60 > 0.49 > 0.42 > 0.38$). Single-step writes
occupy a distinct regime: only about half the facts acquire, but the acquired half barely decays
at all within the stream (retention $0.92$ vs.\ $\approx\!0.55$ at every higher dose). A learning-rate
sweep at fixed single-step dose ($3\times10^{-5}$ to $10^{-3}$) moves the acquisition/retention
split in mirror image and leaves end-state recognition pinned at $0.46$--$0.58$ --- so the
$\approx 0.5$ plateau is invariant to an $8\times$ dose range \emph{and} a $30\times$ step-size
range, which is what one would expect of an interference-limited capacity of the write dynamics
rather than an artifact of any particular setting (at $10^{-3}$ the rank geometry breaks first:
end-state key tier falls to $0.08$ while $2$-AFC still holds --- the tiers even fail at different
stability thresholds). The practical rule is blunt: \textbf{write one step per turn}. The extra
seven steps buy transient recall that the ladder shows will not survive, plus added interference
on every neighbor.

\textbf{Reading the notebook is its own attack surface.} If the weights are the index and the log
is the store, the remaining question is the read path. Three results, three seeds each, on a
timestamped log seeded with adversarial same-attribute distractor lines: (i) on a healthy
(replay-maintained) substrate, top-$k$ impressions retrieve the gold log line for $47/48$ facts
--- including the recognized-but-not-recalled --- so the key tier does function as a retrieval
key; (ii) but injecting retrieved lines into the \emph{adapter-carrying} model's context can pull
already-correct memories off their answers (recalled $42 \to 28$ under the strongest prompt,
seed 1234),
while the frozen base reads the same log cleanly; and (iii) on a deeply forgotten (naked
end-state) substrate the failure inverts --- the base reads the log at $31/18/34$ of $48$
across seeds with an explicit
recency rule while the adapter-carrying model collapses to $2/0/2$, emitting degenerate
recombinations instead of copying any line: unregularized deep forgetting damages the
\emph{read pathway}, not just the content. We stress that (ii) and (iii) used deliberately
adversarial logs; we read them as a provenance rule --- trusted own-log content can be read by
the full model, but the read path is where untrusted content does its damage --- and as a second,
independent cost of unmaintained writing. The capability firewall (\S\ref{sec:firewall}) protects
the weights; nothing in it protects the readout.

\textbf{Does the ladder survive leaving the write form?} Deployment queries are questions, not
the declarative write template, and Act 1's paraphrase collapse warns that expression is
form-bound. Re-measuring the ladder under an interrogative form (``Q: What is the user's
\emph{attr}?'') on the same streams, three seeds per dose: question-form \emph{generation} is
dead ($0/144$ at end-state, both doses) --- the paraphrase collapse is total for emission; the
\emph{key} tier is damped but alive (end-state gold-in-top-$10$ at $\approx 0.7\times$ its
write-form level; single-step writes again give the healthiest question-form keys, $0.42$ vs
$0.28$ at the recall-tuned dose); and $2$-AFC \emph{discrimination generalizes essentially in
full} ($73$ vs $83$ of $144$; half-life $18$ vs $14$) --- confirming, on the timeline, Act 4's
finding that the familiarity signal lives in a question form recall cannot use. We then asked
whether the \emph{workspace} (\S\ref{sec:jspace}) holds the answer under the question form even
when the output surface does not --- and our pinned prediction was wrong: J-projection at the
question's answer slot is monotone in the gold token's rank there (from $0.13$ in-top-$10$ down
to the $0.084$ null floor below rank $30$), and within every rank bucket it is indifferent to
whether $2$-AFC survives. So the form-free discrimination is \emph{not} carried by answer-slot
workspace amplitude; it lives in the comparative, presented-content computation alone. The
division of labor is now measured across surface forms: emission is form-bound, keys are
form-damped, the answer-slot workspace is form-bound (it tracks the production surface, not
recognition), and discrimination --- the presented-mode comparison --- is the one channel that
is form-free.

\textbf{Does the ladder move with model size?} A single-seed rung on a same-family series
(Qwen2.5 $0.5$B/$1.5$B/$3$B/$7$B, hyperparameters fixed; the $7$B in bf16 behind a precision
bridge that matches at end-state) separates two scale effects. Raw capacity moves and then
saturates: end-state recognition $0.50/0.46/0.60$ up to $3$B with key half-life stretching to
$18$ writes and discrimination to $25$ --- consistent with larger pretrained models forgetting
less \citep{ramasesh2022scale} --- but $7$B sits at the $3$B plateau ($0.60$; recall never
durable at any size). What keeps improving is \emph{generalization}: the fraction of write-form
key strength that survives the shift to question form roughly triples from $3$B to $7$B
($0.17 \to 0.48$), and a newer-generation family (Qwen3.5, the $2$/$4$/$9$B rungs) nearly
closes the gap outright --- ratio
$0.72$ at $2$B and $\mathbf{0.95}$ at $4$B, with absolute question-form key coverage rising
across the four rungs ($0.08$ at Qwen2.5-$3$B, $0.21$ at Qwen2.5-$7$B, $0.28$ at
Qwen3.5-$2$B, $0.44$ at Qwen3.5-$4$B, as fractions of the stream) and holding at $0.44$
for both the $4$B and $9$B. The newer $9$B also breaks the old family's capacity plateau
(write-form keys and $2$-AFC both $0.67$, the highest we measure). Question-form
\emph{emission}, by contrast, is dead or nearly so at every size and generation tested ($0/48$
on the newer generation; $1/48$ at $3$B and $7$B on the older) --- the
form-boundness of generation appears near-universal, which the index-and-notebook design never
needed. So the one deployment-relevant weakness the ladder found recedes with both model size
and model generation, while presented-mode discrimination stays form-free everywhere: the windows the design
depends on widen as the base model improves, and the part that widens fastest is exactly the
part that needed it. (One seed per size, fixed adapter rank; a direction, not a law.)

Together the ladder, the dose result, the read-path results, and the form test turn the index
thesis into an operating regime: single-step writes per turn; top-$k$ keys valid for roughly
seven writes unmaintained (or indefinitely under miss-replay), at $\approx 0.7\times$ strength
under deployment-form queries --- an assist to be unioned with question-term search, not a
guarantee; presented-content discrimination valid for roughly sixteen writes and across surface
forms; the log as ground truth; provenance-gated reading. The write policy composes: single-step
writes plus log-driven, error-gated replay reach the same recognition and key ceiling as the
recall-tuned recipe ($2$-AFC $144/144$, key tier $0.99$, three seeds --- the ws1+EWC
arbitration arm; the no-EWC policy arm is $141/144$) at less than half its
gradient budget --- the replay patches exactly the acquisition failures the single step leaves,
because the self-test flags them. The model does remember --- it just needs its notebook.

\subsection{The maintained regime at scale: no wall at 384}\label{sec:marathon}

The ladder's maintenance result --- under error-gated replay the key tier holds at 0.94--1.0
indefinitely --- left one number unmeasured: how far the maintained regime carries when the
policy it selects is simply run, turn after turn, with no night phase. We ran the full day
policy --- one write step plus 4 miss-gated replay steps per turn, replay targets drawn from
the write log --- over a single stream of 384 facts on the 9B substrate ($\sim$1920 optimizer
steps; three seeds). The loop does not hit a wall: cumulative recall is \textbf{0.815--0.849}
(mean 0.828; 326/313/315 of 384), 2-AFC discrimination \textbf{0.995--1.000} (382--384 of 384), and the firewall holds in all three at
$0.74\to0.74$ on 100 GSM8K items (a different frozen 100-item subset --- the
\texttt{gsm8k\_pilot\_ids} items --- from the \texttt{gsm8k\_fw100\_ids} subset behind
\S\ref{sec:firewall}'s $0.79\to0.80$ capability probe: 0.74 and 0.79 are both base scores, on
different item sets). The age curve is flat-to-inverted in all three seeds:
the \emph{oldest} 96 facts sit at 0.844--0.896 while the newest 96 are lowest in every seed
(0.688--0.750) --- a recency deficit from fewer repair passes, not age decay. Per-window repair
demand falls as the pool stabilizes (a declining fraction of facts needs re-writing), and
cumulative recall climbs throughout --- the fixed $O(1)$-per-turn budget is sufficient at this
horizon. The policy carries no
EWC term: at a one-step write dose EWC adds nothing through $n{=}192$; under the earlier
8-step dose it did help at the emission tier --- the dose, not the mechanism, changed.

The reconciliation is mandatory, and it does not restore the ``store'' reading. The 0.83
emission tier is the fixed point of \emph{log-driven repair}: replay targets come
from the ground-truth log, and the periodic probe \emph{is} the harness self-test --- so the
marathon measures the capacity of a maintenance loop, not of the weights. Unmaintained, the
same substrate's emission tier has half-life 2. Two anchors bracket the point. Naked long
streams pin the end-state recognized \emph{fraction} at 0.54/0.57/0.58 across
$n\in\{48,96,192\}$ --- the plateau is a rate, not a count --- with a conditional recognition
half-life of $\approx$140 writes for what survives acquisition; and a maintained $n{=}192$
intermediate run holds recall 0.85 with recognition $\approx$1.0. The full-history self-test
that drives all of this costs $O(n^2)$ cumulative compute over the stream; a deployed schedule
must become spaced --- future work, and a separate paper.

\subsection{Where similarity ranks, experience decides}\label{sec:adjudication}

If finding is increasingly a commodity, what is the in-weight channel for? An adjudication
probe answers. We present four conflicting same-attribute log lines --- one genuinely written,
three never written --- in an \emph{identical} context to every arm, varying only the reader.
The frozen-base reader picks the written line at \textbf{0.21} ($\approx$4-way chance); the
adapter-carrying reader at \textbf{0.58}; with per-line $\dlp$ annotations added, \textbf{0.67}.
This is the one place the in-weight channel is not commoditized --- on the same rig,
full-paraphrase and referential \emph{retrieval} are matched or beaten by an off-the-shelf
embedder (1.00 / 0.96 vs.\ our keys' 0.50 / 0.375). Active memory's value is not finding but
\emph{deciding} --- between conflicting records, and that nothing was experienced at all.
(Single seed, 9B reader, synthetic conflicts: a direction.)

\subsection{The loop, closed: a five-arm end-to-end demo}\label{sec:e2edemo}

question terms, and presents the retrieved timestamped lines back to the \emph{adapter-carrying}

Act 5: \emph{retrieval} is commoditized (an off-the-shelf embedder matches or beats our keys,
\S\ref{sec:adjudication}); \emph{adjudication} among conflicting records is experiential-only
(0.21 base vs.\ 0.58/0.67 with the adapter); and \emph{calibration} --- holding direct-form
fabrication at exactly zero --- is write-history-only (the passive agent below leaks
$\approx$0.1 where the write-carrying agent holds 0.000).

\paragraph{Autonomy: an agent drives the loop.} Everything above is rig-driven: the harness
decides when to scan and what to inject. The last question is whether a model will drive its
own notebook. We gave the marathon-substrate model three tools (GREP, SCAN, CHECK), thinking
enabled, and 128 probes, under a single naive prompt (observational: one symmetric prompt per
arm, no prompt search). On direct forms the active agent reaches accuracy \textbf{0.917} with
fabrication \textbf{0.000} and abstention 0.958 on unwritten queries, against the rig anchor's
1.00/0.083: an autonomy tax of 0.083 on accuracy, with fabrication strictly \emph{better} ---
the agent abstains when the scan lacks gold. On referential forms it reaches \textbf{0.231},
about what the channel physics supplies (pass-1 retrieval 0.282, scan-hit@4 0.205, ideal union
0.410 --- under-exploited); we observed opaque$\to$noun resolution rewrites on 9/39 rows, and
the canonical loop in full: scan $\to$ grep(companion) $\to$ grep(pet) $\to$ CHECK $\to$
correct answer.

The passive control --- frozen base + GREP + an off-the-shelf embedding tool, same protocol ---
sharpens the reading. On direct forms it scores \textbf{1.00} $>$ 0.917 (verbatim-keyword grep
is unbeatable when the keyword is in the question) but fabricates 0.083--0.125 (the two figures
are from different-$n$ runs --- a 12-probe smoke test and the 128-probe full run; the full run
is 0.125) where the active
agent holds 0.000; on referential forms it manages \textbf{0.128} $<$ 0.231, and the mechanism
is \emph{orchestration} failure, not embedding weakness: it called EMBED on 2/39 referential
rows, while the active agent used SCAN on 39/39. A commoditized channel is only commoditized if
the driver uses it --- the impression-owning agent treats ``consult my memory'' as a natural
move; the memoryless one exhausts lexical search first. Token cost: passive 188 vs.\ active 614
per direct query. Scope: one symmetric prompt per arm, observational, not a de-confounded
comparison; a forced-embed-first passive variant would likely lift its referential accuracy
substantially.
The durable claims are two: (1) the experiential zero-argument channel gets used reliably under
naive prompting, and (2) write-history holds direct-form fabrication at exactly zero where the
passive arm leaks $\approx$0.1.

\section{External stress test: the implicit-association wall}\label{sec:inmind}

Everything so far is measured on our own instrument. As a final audit we took the whole design
to an external benchmark built by others to break exactly this kind of system.
\emph{InMind} \citep{inmind2026} tests the \emph{implicit-association blind spot} of agent
memory: each of $125$ tasks pairs one personal fact (``just found out I have a tree-nut
allergy'') with a semantically distant application query (``walk me through a macaron
recipe'') whose correct answer must apply the fact without being asked about it. The published
wall is severe: six external-retrieval memory systems (Mem0, A-Mem, HippoRAG~2, MemoryOS,
xMemory, A-RAG) score $\le 14.4\%$ on indirect application while the backbone
(GPT-5-mini) with the fact placed \emph{in context} scores $84.0\%$; the systems' answer-blind
\emph{target recall} --- did the decisive fact reach the indirect query's context at all ---
is $0.8$--$12\%$. The paper contains no parametric-memory condition; to our knowledge what
follows is the first in-weight arm run on this benchmark, and we ran it as an adversarial
audit of our own thesis: if the index-not-store division of labor is right, the in-weight
side should contribute \emph{nothing} here, and the harness side should be where any gain
lives.

\paragraph{Setup and judging.} We deviate from the benchmark's canonical protocol in one
structural way, disclosed up front: the canonical evaluation isolates each task (memory state =
a 47-session background corpus plus \emph{one} injected fact), whereas we accumulate all $125$
facts into a single adapter and a single log with no background corpus --- the operating
condition of the single-user agent this paper designs for. This makes our store $124$
same-genre distractors rather than $240$ off-genre user turns, changes the retrieval problem in
kind, and means our absolute numbers are not leaderboard entries under their protocol;
we therefore read everything as ratios to our own in-context ceiling. (The canonical-protocol
program later in this section restores per-task isolation --- for the in-weight arms as well
as the frozen ones --- and is leaderboard-comparable in structure, though still on our
backbone and judge.) All $125$ facts are
written by the Act-5 day policy
(single-step writes plus error-gated miss-replay on the $9$B substrate, $r{=}64$), with the
write gate moved to a content-certificate check ($\dlp$ of the written line over a neutral
prefix; the question-form 2-AFC gate is \emph{inverted} on this stream --- binding is the
weakest tier, and these lines are written without question$\to$key bindings --- the
content-vs.-binding lesson again); $125/125$ pass. We deviate from the benchmark's judge
(GPT-5-mini) in two directions and report both: a local Qwen3.6-27B applying the benchmark's
rubric verbatim, and an independent second pass by Claude Fable~5 (Anthropic; greedy decoding,
rubric-sharded) over identical answers; per-item verdicts for both judges are released. Agreement is high on the memory arms ($\kappa$ $0.61$--$0.88$) and low exactly where a
cold audit predicted judge strictness ($\kappa$ $0.39$ on the in-context control; five of six
audit-flagged false negatives flip under the second judge), so we read the pair as a band. The
honesty floor holds under both: with the gold line absent from context, credited application
is $0.008$--$0.028$.

\paragraph{Arms.} The harness contribution is one move: retrieve on the \emph{answer}, not the
question. A frozen-base draft of the answer is embedded (off-the-shelf bge-small) and its
nearest log lines are placed in context for a final answer. Isolation arms then remove or add
the adapter at every site --- drafting, retrieval scoring, risk-term generation, reading ---
so each channel's contribution is measured, not assumed.

\paragraph{The wall stands in the accumulated protocol, and it factors.} No accumulated-protocol
configuration breaks the wall: the best
end-to-end arm reaches $0.27$ of its own in-context ceiling (their best system: $0.17$ of
theirs, under their judge --- ratios, not leaderboard positions; the canonical program
below is where the wall eventually moves). But the wall separates into two measured factors. \emph{Retrieval}: answer-side
embedding triples the naive hit rate ($0.120 \to 0.384$; exact-line matching --- counting
retrieved lexical-twin lines raises it to $0.424$) --- and the gain is semantic, not
lexical ($106/125$ tasks share no content word between gold line and draft; the gain
concentrates there). These numbers live in our accumulated protocol; a canonical-protocol rerun below shows how
much of each is regime-specific. \emph{Dilution}: application of a
\emph{retrieved} gold fact falls monotonically with context size --- $0.80$ with the gold line
alone, $0.79$--$0.80$ in a targeted $\le 6$-line context, $0.52$ at $16$ lines, $0.26$ with
the full log --- so precision and presence trade off, and guaranteed presence (entire log,
$0.258$) still beats top-16 precision end-to-end at this hit rate. The inverted-U in
retrieved-passage count is known \citep{jin2025longrag,cuconasu2024noise}; conditioning on
the gold being \emph{present} zeroes its coverage term, and the decay that remains is
monotone --- the model never benefits from added context, it only sometimes benefits from
added chances at the fact. The two frozen retrieval
routes overlap on only $16/125$ golds (union $0.416$): rank-fusion before pruning is the
obvious next lever, and everything in it is training-free.

\paragraph{Canonical-protocol replication: two regimes, swapped champions.} Because our
accumulated store deviates from the benchmark's per-task isolation, we reran the frozen arms
under the canonical protocol (the benchmark's 486-turn background corpus, of which we index
the $240$ user turns, $+$ one injected fact per task --- a $241$-line store; same judges). The regime flips the rankings. Question-side embedding collapses ($0.016$ retrieval,
$0.000$ application); the answer-side draft loop still \emph{multiplies} retrieval by
$3.5\times$ ($0.016 \to 0.056$; it was $3.2\times$ in the accumulated store --- the
multiplier, not the absolute, is the protocol-invariant claim) but drowns absolutely: verbose
off-genre background matches an advice-style draft better than a terse personal-fact line does
(the gold ranks $110/241$ against a typical draft). What survives the haystack is the
\emph{keyword} front end --- elicit one keyword from the question with the frozen base, embed
it: retrieval $0.152$@6, application $\mathbf{0.144}$, matching the published wall's value
($14.4\%$, their best system --- under our backbone and judges, a coincidence of level
rather than a like-for-like tie), at their best system's relative-to-ceiling number
($0.144/0.84 = 0.171$), with conditional application $0.947$ (above the $\le6$-line
dilution figure because these canonical contexts are smaller still) and a $0.000$ credit floor on
misses. The same keyword route scores only $0.096$ in our accumulated store, where category
keywords drown in same-genre clusters. So retrieval front ends are \emph{regime-specific} ---
draft-embedding owns compact same-genre logs, keyword elicitation owns off-genre haystacks,
both frozen --- and no frozen front end tested to this point breaks the wall in either
regime: the wall's binding constraint everywhere is getting the right line into a small
context, never applying it once there. The program below moves the wall by exactly this
lever.

\paragraph{The canonical program: from the published wall to half the ceiling.} The tie at
the published wall was not the end of the canonical line; a program of thirteen further
chains --- pre-registered arm by arm, with two declared probe amendments after a falsified
target and post-hoc diagnostics labeled as such --- moved it, and the honest accounting of
\emph{what} moved it is the most instructive result in this paper. An agentic loop (the frozen base queries the log,
reads the results, re-queries; $\le 4$ searches) lifts application to $0.176$, with $20$ of
$27$ hits coming from re-queries --- that iterative retrieval beats single-shot is
established \citep{fishing2025iterative}, and our saturation point ($3.3$ searches used regardless
of allowance) replicates the published diminishing-returns knee; the claim we add is the
\emph{substitution} below, not the loop. A per-task in-weight arm --- fresh adapter per task,
write the one fact to a bare-recall criterion, elicit a keyword, retrieve --- reaches
$0.184$, and yields this program's first uncontaminated dose--elicitation gradient (base
keyword $0.152 \to$ recognition-tier $0.160 \to$ recall-tier $0.216$ gold-in-6). Seeding
the agent with the adapter's keyword reaches $0.272$ --- but the attribution control (the
same loop seeded with the \emph{frozen base's} keyword) reaches $0.280$: the two seeds
agree on $92/125$ tasks ($90$ answers byte-identical), one judge flip across those ninety
identical answers prices judge noise at
$\approx 1/90$, and a search-budget sweep ($\{1,2,4,8,12\}$, both arms, ten judged points)
shows the seed advantage is an inverted-U peaking at budget $2$ ($+0.056$) and
statistically zero from budget $4$ on (mean $+0.006$; the $+0.032$ at budget $12$ is
single-arm noise) --- \emph{the retry budget subsumes the write's pointer gain}, while
trebling the budget buys only $0.272 \to 0.320$ at three times the search calls, largely
because the agent self-terminates at $3.3$ searches regardless of allowance. The breakthrough came from a different direction. A two-hop probe
--- the frozen base converts the indirect question into a direct memory-check question, the
adapter answers it, the \emph{answer} is embedded --- failed as first built ($0.184$
retrieval; an echo audit found $19$ of its $23$ ``hits'' were lexical echoes of the probe
question and genuine at-probe recall was $3.2\%$), and the failure localized the lock: the
generated question addressed the user as ``you'', off the trained form. Re-voicing the hop
question \emph{as the user would ask it} (``Did I tell you about\ldots'') unlocked genuine
recall to $32.8\%$ and retrieval to $0.464$. (This lock is not the reversal curse
\citep{berglund2024reversal}: the argument order never changes, only the speech-act
register; and the off-form refusals are the learned-default-response behavior of
\citet{kang2024unfamiliar}, not an unexplained attractor.) A $2{\times}2$ factorial (who
writes the question $\times$ who answers it) attributes the entire effect to the answering pathway at
the single-fact tier (question-knowledge cells are indistinguishable from base there, and
become nonzero only once the adapter holds a real stream), and the recognition-tier probe
is dead in every cell --- certify at $2$ steps, cash at $6$, where we name a per-fact dose
by the readout tier it reaches; the Act-5 single-step rule reaches the recall dose
\emph{across} turns via miss-gated replay, where these per-task arms reach it in one
sitting. Cashed to answers, the two
front ends bracket the in-weight contribution exactly: embedding the frozen base's
user-voiced question alone scores $0.312$ --- the frozen top (shared with the budget-8
frozen-seed agentic arm), from genre match with the
log's own first-person register, no training anywhere. That generated text out-retrieves
the query is HyDE \citep{gao2023hyde}, and that the gain is a question/declarative
\emph{style} gap is the stated thesis of HyPE \citep{vake2026hype}; we re-claim neither.
What is ours is the placement: on a per-user append-only log, index-time question
generation is unavailable, so the register match must be manufactured at query time --- and
the best probe of all is produced by the adapter that already holds the memory, parametric
memory serving as a \emph{query generator} rather than an answer source --- and the adapter's hop answer
scores $0.360$ ($+0.048$ over its own instrument control; directionally positive,
underpowered at $n{=}125$, sign test $p{=}0.22$). The last step came from realism
itself: writing the task's $41$-turn background stream \emph{into the same adapter} before
the fact --- the deployment condition, not an ablation --- improves every readout. The
free-form statement probe, dead in isolation ($0.136$ retrieval, $0.71$ refusal rate),
reaches $0.576$ with refusals at $0.12$. Mixing diverse data to unlock extraction is
established offline \citep{allenzhu2024,chen2024remix,sun2025mixed}, and benign
fine-tuning eroding refusals is a known safety result, read here in the opposite sign
\citep{qi2023finetune}; what is ours is that the \emph{incidental} data a deployment
already produces supplies the benefit online, uncurated, for free. And the simplest
pipeline in the program --- ask
the adapter to state its best guess about the user, embed that one sentence, retrieve,
serve cold --- reaches $\mathbf{0.448}$ application: $0.53$ of the benchmark's published
in-context ceiling ($0.448/0.84$; $0.56$ of our own measured $0.80$), from the $0.171$
relative wall where the published systems stand --- with two caveats banked when the arm
was designed: part of this gain is the index learning the \emph{register} of the very
store it retrieves from, since the written chatter is also in the log; and at design time
no frozen control existed under the real-stream write protocol, so the step was first
recorded as attributed to neither instrument nor memory. A pre-registered control pair
run after review (2026-08-14) settles the attribution: the identical statement pipeline
with the gold write removed --- once with no writes at all, once with the 51 chatter
writes but no gold --- collapses to $0.080$ retrieval and $0.080$ application in both
arms, with the statement a byte-identical ``I don't remember'' refusal on $125/125$
tasks in each. Register-learning without the fact does not reach the embedding stage;
the register assist cashes only conditional on the fact being written, and the last step
belongs to the write. The scope of that attribution matters: frozen retrieval did not
collapse --- the frozen question front end stands at $0.384/0.312$, and log-only
harnesses do memory well; what the controls kill is the register-echo reading of this
pipeline, whose statement form works only written (frozen statement $0.080$ vs frozen
question $0.384$). Measured against the best frozen front end on the paired judged
outcomes, the write's cashed end-to-end value is $+0.136$ ($32{:}15$ discordant tasks,
exact McNemar $p{=}0.019$; a post-hoc test --- the pre-registered lines were the
control collapses). The frozen maximum $0.312$ is shared by the budget-8 frozen-seed
agentic arm, and the margin survives that comparator too ($35{:}18$, $p{=}0.027$).
Subsequent writes
break the form lock; the hop machinery, built to work around that lock, adds nothing once
it is broken ($0.440$). A post-hoc comparison across three write schedules points to
\emph{post-fact interleaving} as the enabling condition (dose unmeasured) --- the
fresh-write over-commitment it relaxes is Editing Overfit \citep{zhang2025editingoverfit},
and unlocking already-memorized content with later meaningful training is reported at
training time \citep{wu2026rote}; the retro-active release of an \emph{earlier} locked
fact by \emph{subsequent unrelated} writes is the part we have not found stated: probed
immediately after the fact's writes the statement readout is locked (instance rate
$0.06$--$0.08$; retrieval $0.136$ in isolation) regardless of what preceded them, and
writes that \emph{follow} the fact anneal it open (retrieval $0.576$ at $n{=}125$;
$0.625$ in an $n{=}8$ environment-regression rerun) --- an ordering deployment provides
for free, since users keep talking after the fact and before the question.

\paragraph{The certification--retrieval dose wall, and what the key is made of.} The
recognition thesis owed one more measurement: can the \emph{discriminative} channel
retrieve --- rank the log by the adapter-vs-base likelihood shift $\dlp$ --- at the
two-step dose where the certificate turns positive? The instrument should be named for
what it is: a reference-calibrated likelihood ratio with the pre-write base as the
reference --- the standard membership-inference signal \citep{carlini2022lira}, which is
known to work on fine-tuning data specifically \citep{duan2024mia} --- repurposed as a
read head; and the recognition-before-recall ordering it probes was reported for few-shot
fine-tuned memories by \citet{orhan2023recognition} (recognition at ${\sim}1$ exposure,
recall at ${\sim}3$, recognition the more interference-robust), with the
likelihood-improves-while-recall-stays-zero gap recently re-documented for one-step
test-time LoRA \citep{song2026beyondppl}. What follows is that ordering put on a
controlled dose axis --- and $\dlp$ is not treated as recall anywhere in this paper: the
gap between the index and what can be emitted is the object of study. A first attempt returned a perfect
score that we voided ourselves: with only the fact ever written, the scan string is
byte-identical to the training string and the $240$ background lines are untouched, so the
probe measures training-set membership, and a perfect score is the null. Under the
real-stream condition the answer inverts: at the recognition dose the buried fact ranks
near the \emph{bottom} of the store ($236/241$ median; $0.000$ gold-in-6), because the
register-generalization that helps the generative route hurts here --- the $41$ chatter
writes lift $\dlp$ on every same-register line, written or not, and the two-step needle
drowns in its own stream's spillover. Retrieval through the scan exists only at the recall
dose ($0.640$, median rank $2$), the same dose the generative route needs: \emph{there is
no cheap discriminative shortcut; certification and retrieval are separated by a dose
wall}, and recognition's robust role is presence certification --- given the candidate
string --- not store-side search. What the scan discriminates is also not meaning but
form: a held-out paraphrase of the fact scores $0.000$/$0.064$ at either dose under
verbatim writes. But the key is the \emph{write's} artifact, not the mechanism's: writing
a fresh paraphrase each round inverts it --- the held-out paraphrase rises to $0.600$
while the original string falls to $0.184$ --- the index keys on whatever surface family
the writes exercised, bidirectionally. That augmentation enables extraction is the
Physics-of-LMs result \citep{allenzhu2024}; the inversion --- the original surface's own
standing \emph{falling} as the exercised family's rises, read off a held-out-surface
likelihood index rather than QA accuracy --- is, to our knowledge, not in that literature,
and it upgrades ``write format gates extraction'' to ``write format \emph{selects the
key}''. Burial, finally, separates content from dose. Forty
subsequent one-step chatter writes kill nothing above an eight-step natural-language fact
(recall $0.92$--$0.95$ at $n{=}125$ across both write regimes; certificates $1.00$ with
\emph{rising} margins; scan $0.90/0.66$), and an $n{=}8$ same-register smoke replicates
the direction; this appears to contradict the Act-5 stream until
one controls content: the Act-5 facts were unguessable pseudowords by design, and
rerunning the burial with pseudoword facts under the identical harness kills recall in most
of the stream ($31\%$ alive by the stem criterion; $15\%$ by strict value production)
while every certificate survives (all $125$ burial tasks, margins rising from $3.8$ to
$5.2$ nats through the burial) and the scan still returns the fact in the top six
($1.000$; the competitor set is the $41$ written background lines plus $199$ unwritten
ones --- the burial facts are not store candidates). The surviving generations show the mechanism in the failures themselves:
syllable-level value corruption, competitors' syllables invading the emitted value
(``roptavrop'' for \emph{roptavvor}), fluent in structure and wrong in content --- while
discrimination is untouched. The recognition-without-recall residue of Act~2
reappears exactly where the paper first found it --- on content with no pretraining
prior --- replicating \citet{orhan2023recognition}'s interference asymmetry with a
calibrated instrument (and adding the counterintuitive detail that the surviving margins
\emph{rise} under burial), and with a mechanism visible in the failures that
accuracy-only metrics are structurally unable to see: interference corrupts the
\emph{emission sequence} into syllable-level chimeras of competing values; it does not
erase the \emph{discrimination trace}. Content is
sufficient to produce the gap under this one fixed harness --- the Acts 1--5 streams also
differ in write form and criterion, which we did not separate (the CounterFact contrast,
Appendix~\ref{app:cf}, points the same way) --- so the pseudoword streams are the
adversarial floor, and natural personal facts, riding priors, sit several-fold higher on
the same axis. Retention under sequential writes is already known to depend on
presentation breadth \citep{oneill2026continual} and dataset provenance
\citep{chen2024remix}; the axis isolated here is \emph{lexical/entity familiarity} under a
matched template and matched interference --- distinct from propositional prior-conflict,
where the sign can invert \citep{oneill2026continual} --- and it is large enough to
account for the apparent contradiction between the pseudoword-stream and natural-fact
literatures.

\paragraph{The in-weight audit: zero at every serve-time channel.} The adapter that passed
$125/125$ write certificates contributes nothing to this benchmark's score \emph{at serve
time}, channel by channel (its one cashable contribution, quantified above, is on the
query side). As a \emph{retriever}, familiarity ranks the log at exactly chance ($0.032 = 4/125$) ---
and a post-release audit sharpened the reading: the ranking is \emph{degenerate}, collapsing
onto a length-driven per-line prior that the neutral-prefix subtraction fails to remove (one
line occupies $89/125$ of the top-4 slots). Either way, familiarity is not relevance. As a \emph{drafter}, adapter
drafts surface zero bridge entities (gold-content-word overlap with the target fact is
identical to base drafts under every filter we tried), and a teacher-forced token-tier probe finds no sub-emission elevation either
(median bridge-token boost $4.57$ vs.\ matched-null $4.52$, null 95th percentile $7.78$;
$n{=}103$ after excluding tasks whose bridge words already appear in the base draft). As a
\emph{risk enumerator}, frozen-base risk terms retrieve as well as adapter terms ($0.160$ vs.\
$0.152$). As a \emph{reader}, the adapter is \emph{negative}: on identical retrieved lines the
frozen base scores $0.216$ vs.\ the adapter's $0.136$ (paired discordants $12{:}2$,
McNemar $p \approx 0.013$), and the mechanism is the Act-3 accent --- adapter answers are
$44\%$ shorter (median $725$ vs.\ $1286$ characters), and warnings are what gets cut. A
distribution audit rules out the two obvious artifacts: no repeated answer string scores on
more than one task in either arm, and score is uncorrelated with length ($r{=}-0.01$; the
frozen reader's \emph{longest} quartile scores worst) --- the mediator is gold-mention, the
frozen reader writing the retrieved fact into its answer $24{:}5$ over the adapter reader. Two
escalations were attempted --- and a post-release instrument audit \emph{voided both}, which we
report as the self-refutation it is. A fresh $24$-fact subset was written ``to the recall
criterion'' (direct-QA forms, per-fact refresh before probing), but the criterion check turned
out not to measure recall: the assistant persona answers the direct question with a privacy
refusal (``I don't have access to your personal information\ldots'') in $20/24$ cases, and the
substring test then matched query-echoed and refusal-vocabulary words, so the ``recall-alive''
population was mostly false positives (one clean case in $24$). A thinking-mode arm
(1{,}024-token chains) compounded a denominator mismatch --- and under matched populations its
direction actually reverses (chains mention bridge words $9/24$ adapter-on vs.\ $6/24$ off,
recall-tier arm) --- while the stored transcripts are too truncated to re-score. We then rebuilt
the instrument (a persona prompt that legitimizes memory answers with an honest ``I don't
remember'' exit, write-format aligned to the probe format, fabrication controls, stem matching)
and reran the escalation. Under the valid instrument the recall criterion is reachable
($16$--$17$ of $24$ alive after excluding stem-matching false positives, zero refusals). An
apparent gray-zone signal at the indirect keyword position ($5/18$ adapter-on vs.\ $1/18$
off) dissolved under a distribution audit: the credits ride on sub-word fragments --- the
token `I', a first-person write-accent marker, sits in the adapter's top-10 for $21/24$
tasks --- and a whole-token-plus-promiscuity gate reduces the contrast to $1$--$2$ vs.\ $0$;
a matching $+3$ direction in thinking chains dissolves the same way. So the defensible
statement is uniform across tiers: no credible surfacing of the indirect association at any
tier we can write to, with probes that bound only large effects. (Both this null and the
wide-readout fan's below are properties of the accumulated protocol and its off-form queries; the canonical
program above shows the same channel moving retrieval under per-task isolation with a
user-voiced query --- and those are different measurements: indirect-association
\emph{bridging} here, direct memory-check \emph{recall} there.) All six recall
``failures'' turn out to be grading artifacts of our accumulated protocol: the benchmark's
direct queries repeat across tasks ($14$ tasks share $4$ question forms --- e.g.\ two health
conditions and a pregnancy all answer ``What health conditions do I have?''), which is
unambiguous under the canonical per-task isolation but ill-posed under accumulation, where a
deterministic single answer can satisfy only one task's gold. On the well-posed items the
recall criterion holds for all but the stem-artifact cases ($\ge 16$ of $18$); the never-written controls answer ``I don't remember''
cleanly ($0/8$ fabrication). The honest residue is an ambiguity-handling contrast: asked an
ambiguous question, the in-weight channel emits one fact with no signal that others exist,
while the log returns \emph{all} matching lines and lets the reader see the ambiguity. A final wide-readout arm closes the attribution: harvesting the
top-100 tokens of the keyword distribution and embedding each (rather than trusting the
greedy keyword) doubles the \emph{frozen} retriever ($0.080 \to 0.160$ gold-in-context; union
with answer-side embedding $0.432$) while the adapter's contribution to the same fan is
$-0.032$ --- instance words do live in the distribution's deep tail, but they come from world
knowledge, equally with the adapter off. The teacher-forced token-tier probe stands as a null
but an underpowered one (first-token-fragment matching deflates sensitivity), so it bounds
only large effects.

\paragraph{What the stress test pins.} At deployment dose, on this substrate, facts become
behavior only by transiting the context window: in-weight-only serving sits at the honesty
floor ($\approx 0.01$--$0.03$) against our measured in-context ceiling of $0.80$, and every
end-to-end gain in the canonical program --- from the published $0.171$ relative wall to
$0.53$ of the benchmark's published ceiling ($0.56$ of our own) --- was won on the
\emph{query side} of that transit, never by emitting facts from
weights at serve time. The refined division of labor is sharper than the one we started
with. The write's cashable value is as a \emph{query sharpener}: an adapter that holds the
fact answers a user-voiced memory-check question with the fact's own words, and that
sentence is a better retrieval key than anything the frozen base can synthesize --- worth
$+0.048$ end-to-end over the identical frozen machinery (consistent but underpowered);
the write's \emph{keyword-level pointer}, by contrast, is worth nothing once an agentic
loop may simply re-query, and whether the sharpener itself survives an agentic reader is
untested. The rest of the climb was
instrument: genre match (a first-person question out-retrieves every trained front end we
built), realism (writes that follow the fact anneal the readout open; deployment ordering
provides this for free), and precision (short contexts, because application decays
monotonically in context size). The operating rule survives every escalation intact ---
\emph{serve cold, write hot} --- generate for the user with the frozen base, keep the
adapter for certification, query sharpening, and adjudication (store-side familiarity
search is retired below the recall dose --- the dose wall); and the write protocol is a
design surface, not a fixed cost: what you write (paraphrase or verbatim) chooses the
index's key, and when you write (facts should not be the last thing written before they
are needed) chooses whether the readout is open at all.
Scope: one benchmark, one $9$B substrate, an off-the-shelf small embedder; the accumulated
arms' non-original judges are reported as a band, and the canonical-program arms are
single-judge (judge~2 only); whether a much larger model's reasoning could pull weak
in-weight traces into its chain (an ``aha'' channel) is unmeasured anywhere, including
here.

\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{The design, stated once.} A lifelong in-weight memory system should be \emph{log-backed}
(recitation poisons), \emph{self-testing} (the latent class is invisible to recency policies and
cheap to harvest --- from the log, with self-test cadence and self-test/readout form alignment
as first-class budget items), \emph{day-only by default} (single-step writes plus miss-gated
replay make the day loop itself a continuous error-gated micro-consolidation; a separate night
phase is optional rather than structural, \S\ref{sec:marathon}), \emph{single-adapter at
serving time} (never two adapters concurrently --- the two-adapter hot mount is what taxes
fluency and old-fact recall at read time, \S\ref{sec:firewall}), and --- Act 4's addition ---
\emph{scan-at-the-boundary}: the recognition channel is consulted on every question
\emph{whose targets have reached the recall dose}, as
a ranked retrieval assist over external content --- the stress test's dose wall is the
gate: below that dose the scan carries no retrieval signal under realistic streams
(\S\ref{sec:inmind}) --- never as a fire/no-fire gate (the
write-form slot-KL gate of \S\ref{sec:gate} remains the one surviving in-form exception; no
question-form gate survives), and never on
the adapter's own generations; calibrated abstention downstream handles never-written content.
Every clause is a measured comparison, not a preference. The adapter that
results is not a transcript; it is an index over a log --- in the strongest configurations it
recognizes 100\% of the day while recalling 85--96\%, and the recognition surplus is exactly
what re-grounding against the log exploits.

\textbf{Nights, when run.} Demoting the night phase to optional does not leave it lawless; the
two laws are measured. (i) Nights must be \emph{miss-gated}: in a pre-registered smoke test,
verbatim massed re-writing of already-held facts was destructive (recall\textsubscript{w}
$0.50\to0.083$, 2-AFC $0.792\to0.583$) --- the same dose inversion the ladder found, and the
mechanism behind misslog's win. (ii) Whatever \emph{is} replayed should be paraphrase-diverse:
diversity buffered the interference and moved question-form readouts up while write-form went
down --- an implicit$\to$explicit trade (small $n$), and the same trade the stress test's
key-inversion result reproduces at scale, where paraphrase writes lift held-out-paraphrase
retrieval to $0.600$ as the verbatim string falls to $0.184$ (\S\ref{sec:inmind}). A methodological law belongs beside these:
familiarity must be measured as written-minus-unwritten \emph{separation}, never as a YES-rate
--- naive replay raises YES on unwritten items too ($0.42\to0.75\to0.92$).

\textbf{What the steady state means.} The throttled regime --- protect the stock, squeeze the
intake --- is what bounded consolidation \emph{should} do; it is the stability--plasticity
trade-off surfacing at the systems level. It implies a design obligation: intake throttling must
be made visible (the system's newest knowledge is its weakest), e.g.\ by routing
recent-information queries to the day adapter or the log. The loss-of-plasticity line
\citep{kong2026plasticity} addresses the same trade-off inside one network --- grow fresh
random units, prune dead ones at task boundaries --- and our core-plus-day adapter design
is its plug-in-level discrete analogue (mounting a fresh day adapter is growth, retiring
one is elastic pruning, the pinned core is protected capacity), with one asymmetry worth
naming: a retired adapter loses
nothing, because content lives in the log; pruned units are gone. The two measurement axes
are complementary --- that line asks whether the network can \emph{still learn}; this paper
asks whether what it learned can \emph{still be read out} --- and our central failure mode
(writes still succeeding, certificate positive, off-form readout locked) would not register
in that framework's readouts.

\textbf{The tension with self-synthesized rehearsal.} \citet{huang2024ssr} show self-synthesized
rehearsal \emph{mitigates} forgetting for skills; our recite and hybrid arms show it
\emph{poisons} for atomic facts. Rehearsal of self-generated data is safe exactly to the extent
that the generator is still a good model of the target distribution: for broad skills a fluent
sample is usually correct; for a specific binding it is correct only if the binding survived.
This predicts self-synthesized rehearsal degrades with target granularity --- testable with the
released loop.

\textbf{For theory.} A first-order scale-invariance argument says per-write ``gentleness''
cannot create accumulation (shrinking the step shrinks a fact's margin and its interference
proportionally); only write \emph{geometry} and \emph{re-instatement schedules} can. Our
mechanism ladder walks exactly those two levers; meta-learned fast-weight memories
\citep{ahn2025} can be read as the activation-state solution to the same problem.

\textbf{A note on provenance.} An earlier position paper by the author \citep{lin2026mathlife}
specified five criteria for a mechanism that
would maintain a network's structure under continued learning --- locality, fast protective
action under a
slow regulatory strategy, topology preservation, threat-targeted selectivity, and closed-loop
homeostatic
regulation --- and ruled out elastic weight consolidation on all five as a static, global,
topology-oblivious
wrapper rather than a self-maintenance mechanism. That paper's own instantiation of the
criteria did not
survive its own experiments: $\beta_1$ was falsified both as a capability gauge and as a
mediator of
forgetting, which left the criteria standing but with no measured signal to drive them. The
loop measured here
is a \emph{behavioral} successor to that falsified instantiation: probe failure stands in for
the topological
damage signal the earlier framework could not compute, error-gated replay is the targeted
repair, and the
maintained regime sustains 384 online writes at cumulative recall 0.82--0.85 (three seeds) with a
flat-to-inverted
age curve. We claim a partial, behavioral analogue and not an instantiation --- our writes are
backpropagated
rather than local, the invariant maintained is expression rather than manifold topology, and
the horizon is
hours of streaming rather than the years those criteria were written for.

\textbf{For the unlearning debate.} Erasure and residual traces are both real and separable by a
cheap 2-AFC meter; arguments about whether forgetting ``really'' deletes should condition on the
write-time protection regime. And 2-AFC discriminability is \emph{not} evidence of
recoverability: claims of ``hidden but recoverable'' knowledge should be held to a savings or
relearning test, not a recognition test alone.

\textbf{What would falsify the thesis.} A consolidation policy that beats misslog at matched
budget without a log; a horizon at which the throttled state collapses rather than plateaus; a
substrate where recitation is high-fidelity enough to avoid poisoning; an error-gated gain that
vanishes under measurement-independent self-tests stronger than our interrogative control; a
candidate-free readout of familiarity that closes the gap to presented-mode AUC (which would
promote the index back toward a store); or a familiarity signal that false-fires on plausible
never-experienced content at scale. All are runnable with the released instrument.

\paragraph{Two open problems our measurements pin down.}\label{sec:openproblem}
\emph{(i) The dynamical recall law.} The rank-64 adapter has roughly 67M trainable parameters;
at the $\sim$2 bits per parameter knowledge capacity reported for batch training
\citep{allenzhu2024capacity}, its \emph{static} capacity is orders of magnitude beyond 48 facts
--- yet naked sequential SGD sustains only a $\sim$1.5-batch FIFO. The binding constraint is
dynamical, not capacitive. The well-posed question is the continuous recall law $R(m;\, d, r,
\varepsilon, \eta)$ --- the fraction of $m$ near-orthogonal atoms still expressible after being
written by sequential SGD, at learning rate $\eta$ and target precision $\varepsilon$, into a
rank-$r$ adapter subspace of a $d$-dimensional representation --- together with the steady-state
conditions of an error-gated re-instatement loop. The static side is classical
(Johnson--Lindenstrauss-type packing says the codes fit); whether the dynamical law saturates
that bound, falls polynomially short, or is governed by a different quantity is open --- we do
not present saturation as the only alternative. Any candidate theory must also fit our savings
null. \emph{(ii) Recognition without metacognition.} Presented-mode recognition at AUC
0.93--0.99 coexists, in the same weights, with a fire/no-fire readout that four independent
instruments fail to extract (\S\ref{sec:readoutgap}): candidate-scored $\dlp$ (self-argmax
bias); the slot statistic out of form ($J = 0.27$ on the calibration seed --- though the same
threshold family reaches $J = 0.35$ and $0.50$ on the held-out seeds, so the bound is a
calibration-seed statement, not a ceiling); the leave-one-out presented-mode statistic as a
gate (AUC 0.63/0.38); and the model's own verbalized expectation (separation exactly zero,
with the prompt-attribution matrix showing the self-report to be a pure framing echo). What
computation would let a system \emph{query its own familiarity} without being shown the answer
--- and is the gap fundamental to SGD-written low-rank memory or an artifact of our probes? A
native per-input familiarity scalar --- for example, the goodness of Forward-Forward-style
training \citep{hinton2022ff} --- would test whether honest nescience is a backprop artifact
rather than something fundamental. A related signal question sits behind both problems: the
signal that gates repair here --- a probe miss --- is coarse and \emph{lagging}, firing only at
scheduled points and only once expression is already lost. \citet{lin2026mathlife} posed the
same detector in topological form and falsified its own candidate; whether a cheaper or earlier
damage signal exists is that open problem, restated in behavioral coordinates. We
release the bench --- per-fact raw timelines at both timescales plus the familiarity probes ---
as a direct testbed any candidate answer must fit.

\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limits}

\textbf{Scale and power.} The main audits run on a 2B primary and a 1.7B transfer substrate,
one hyperparameter setting (tuned on the first); the scale study adds single-seed rungs at
0.5/1.5/3/7B on the older generation and 2/4/9B on the newer, and the 9B carries the marathon,
the adjudication probe, both agent arms, and the InMind audit. Per-substrate magnitudes do not transfer and are
floors. 1--5 seeds per condition on
nondeterministic ROCm hardware, no significance testing; several orderings (\qwen{} plain-replay
miss vs.\ uniform; the form-independent gate's margin over uniform; GRACE paraphrase) are
directions with overlapping seeds and are labeled as such. The 30-day horizon arms are $n{=}2$;
the D6 policy comparison is $n{=}3$ (2 on the second substrate). The marathon, adjudication,
and agent results are single seed (the marathon itself is three seeds: 0.849/0.815/0.820, recognition 0.995--1.000, firewall +0.00 in all three, age inversion in all three) --- every such number is labeled inline, and the marathon is
kept localized (\S\ref{sec:marathon}) so replication seeds can update it in place.

\textbf{Streams and probes.} The main stream is synthetic and collision-free; CounterFact adds
real entities but not \emph{revision} or conflict, untested at both timescales; the InMind audit
adds externally authored personal facts and an external rubric, at $n{=}125$, single seed,
non-original judges reported as a band. Within the canonical program specifically: the
$+0.048$ query-sharpening margin is underpowered (sign test $p{=}0.22$; judge noise anchored
at one flip per $90$ identical answers) and is reported as a consistent direction, not a
finding; the real-stream gains partly reflect the index learning the \emph{register} of the
very store it retrieves from (the written chatter is also in the log --- by design, but a
transfer caveat); the reader model is unstable to distractor composition (one to three
verdict flips between arms retrieving the same gold), which caps how much any retrieval
margin can cash; and one mid-program library upgrade on the rig was bracketed by a
regression rerun (forward-pass probes replicate exactly; generation-path probes replicate
on the checked protocol) rather than by full re-execution. The content-dependence result
bounds the program's headline in both directions: the $0.53$ is measured on natural personal
facts, and the same harness on pseudoword content loses most of its recall under burial ---
the number does not transfer across content classes, and Acts 1--5's pseudoword walls do
not transfer to natural-fact deployments either. 24 facts per day
is a toy day; the generator (784 unique attributes) was exhausted before the system was, so the
capacity knee is unlocated. Recall probes are form-matched to the writes; the paraphrase probe
quantifies the resulting inflation and the form-independent self-test control quantifies the
coupled share of the error-gating gain ($\sim$5/48), but paraphrase-based training targets were
not explored. The savings probe is $k\le2$ on one substrate; the hostile reading of the \smol{}
EWC arm ($\lambda$ mis-scaling freezing the adapter) is not excluded. The 10-item firewall is
near-trivial on \smol{} (base 0.40); the 100-item probe covers core-only serving on the 2B
loop, the hot two-adapter \textsc{core+day} state has only NLL and 10-item readings, and the
hot \emph{single-adapter} state is measured at 100-item resolution on the 9B marathon substrate
only ($0.79\to0.80$, single seed). Recognition margins use same-stream distractors; margins near zero are
not calibrated confidence. Free-form dialogue quality was not scored.

\textbf{Act 4--5 scope.} The familiarity results are inference-only probes on saved day-scale
adapters: synthetic facts, one strong and one weak adapter family, 3 seeds each.
The gate demo is a minimal existence-and-failure demonstration, not a system: fabrication is
measured on-distribution (never-written attributes from the same generator), not on public
hallucination benchmarks. Fabrication figures are \emph{direct-form}: under opaque referential
questions with a mismatched retrieved context the unwritten-query rate rises to 0.71 in the
forced-context probe and 0.235 in the agent loop --- the content-vs-binding hole
(\S\ref{sec:readoutgap}) is the scope boundary of the authentication claims. Multi-turn agent
behavior is now probed, but only observationally (a single prompt per arm,
\S\ref{sec:e2edemo}); systematic multi-turn evaluation, behavioral compliance with familiarity
annotations,
error-familiarity (does familiarity fire on \emph{corrected} facts?), and adaptive-RAG baselines
\citep{flare2023,seakr2024,skr2023} remain future work. Slot-KL's written-side coverage (52--56\%)
leaves half the written queries unexploited, and it is in-form (cloze) only; the candidate-free
readout gap is an open problem,
not a solved engineering detail.

\textbf{Design space.} A minimal GRACE-style arm was run on the day-scale stream; WISE and
MEMOIR were not, and no editor was run inside the loop. One nightly budget and one day/core rank
pair; the throttled steady state's location presumably moves with both. The misslog self-test
costs generation time linear in history; whether sampled self-tests preserve the steady state is
untested. Pre-registration dates (2026-07-08, 2026-07-11, 2026-07-13, 2026-07-15) are
self-reported in-source rather than externally timestamped; externally DOI-stamped
pre-registration is adopted for future runs. The final sprint added ledger pre-registrations on
2026-07-27 through 2026-07-30: F6 v3/v3.1, the agentic end-to-end demo (\#18), active-vs-passive
(\#19), the night smoke test (\#20), and the marathon (\#21 --- the last with its probe-schedule
confound declared before the result). The agent results of \#18/\#19 are observational --- a
single prompt per arm --- and are labeled as such wherever quoted.

\section{Conclusion}

Ask a LoRA adapter to be a fact store, and it fails in a structured, reproducible way:
persistence is not accumulation --- facts flicker within a handful of writes under every
mechanism, and capacity is set by re-instatement; expression binds to the write form and
collapses under paraphrase; and what protected forgetting leaves behind supports 2-AFC
discrimination but, by our own savings probe, neither expression nor faster relearning. Ask what
survives, and the answer is the same across SGD, a key--value editor, depth bands, and
substrates: recognition --- the last readout to die. Run the system for a month of simulated
days and the consequence is architectural: expressed truth must live in a log (skip it and the
system poisons itself with its own fluent errors), consolidation must target what the system
itself fails, and two adapters must never serve concurrently. And the recognition that survives turns
out to be the key to using that log: read candidate-free over presented content, it separates
experienced from never-experienced at AUC 0.93--0.99 across a form change that kills recall,
sits at chance for the base by construction, cuts direct-form fabrication when wired into even a minimal
answer-independent gate --- and resists being read out unprompted: four independent instruments
find the same one-way channel, recognized when shown yet silent when asked, and that asymmetry
is the open problem we
close on. Run the design itself, finally, and it holds: single-step writes plus error-gated
replay maintain a 384-fact stream at cumulative recall 0.815--0.849 with a flat-to-inverted age curve
(three seeds), an adapter-carrying reader adjudicates conflicting records where a frozen one
sits at chance, and an agent drives the whole loop unassisted --- forgetting is a property of
the write regime and the content, not the substrate. Take the design to an external
benchmark built to break
it --- InMind's implicit-association wall, in what is to our knowledge its first
parametric-memory arm --- and the audit returns the same verdict from the outside, then
sharpens it: the wall factors into retrieval $\times$ dilution and then \emph{moves}, from
the published $0.171$ of the benchmark's published ceiling to $0.53$, almost all of it on
the query side of the
context transit --- genre-matched first-person questions, short precise contexts, writes
that follow the fact --- with the write's own cashed margin small and consistent, and the
in-weight channel serving best by not generating --- serve cold, write hot.
The weights are an index; the log is the store; the design follows. All of it is
invisible to aggregate accuracy; the per-fact, per-night instrument --- together with the
self-run refutations of our own stronger readings --- is the contribution we most hope others
reuse. The model does remember --- it just needs its notebook.

Restricting the adapter to matched-capacity 8-layer bands (Table~\ref{tab:layers}) yields a
monotone depth gradient in \emph{recall}: the early band recalls almost nothing under every
mechanism; the late band alone matches full-stack replay recall (21.3 vs.\ 19.0, with the
tightest seed triple in the study, 21/22/21) at a third of the parameters, and it is the only
band in which EWC's residual-trace-and-recovery behavior exists at all (29\% vs.\ 0--2\%).
Restricting to the middle band --- the region favored by locate-then-edit knowledge editing
\citep{meng2022rome,meng2023memit} and by RL layer-contribution analyses \citep{onelayer2026}
--- \emph{hurts} recall under every protected mechanism.

Recognition changes the interpretation. The mid band's replay arm \emph{recognizes} 42.7 of 48
--- essentially full-stack level (43.3) --- while \emph{recalling} 11.0 of 48, and the late-band
composition recognizes 48/48/47 while recalling 26: restricted bands can \emph{store} (in the
bounded, 2-AFC sense of \S\ref{sec:savings}) nearly everything and \emph{express} a fraction of
it. The early band is a genuine (if partial) storage desert: its protected-arm recognition
(28--36) sits closest to chance. The regime contrast with batch injection \citep{back2026lora}
is therefore a decomposition, not a contradiction: \textbf{single-pass online writes can deposit
discriminable bindings at many depths, but greedy recall of them is readout-proximal};
multi-epoch batch training, which can afford to build early-layer features and re-extract them
over many passes, faces no such expression bottleneck. Whether batch-trained early-layer
memories show the same storage/expression split is an open question our instrument could answer
in their regime.

\section{Benna--Fusi cascade details}\label{app:bf}

The BF row of Table~\ref{tab:main} applies the Benna--Fusi multi-timescale synaptic cascade
\citep{benna2016} to the adapter parameters. Its numbers are from pre-recognition-meter runs
(3-seed finals 4/3/5, mean 4.0, median first-miss 5, recovery 10\%; the released fine-probe pair
gives 3/6, recovery 10\%). The rescue sweep is flat: across $dt\in\{0.1,0.5\}$ and cascade depth
$\{8,32\}$ (2 seeds each), finals stay at 2--6/48 --- the cascade stretches the initial glide of
a written cohort but provides no re-instatement, and without re-instatement there is no
accumulation (\S\ref{sec:reinstatement}). BF's flatness against EWC's accumulation is itself a
dissociation (protection alone is not what EWC contributes here), but BF hyperparameters were
not exhaustively tuned for this regime and we do not claim the dissociation beyond the swept
range.

\section{GRACE-style editing baseline: full details}\label{app:grace}

We implement a GRACE-style discrete key--value adaptor (the mechanism follows
\citet{hartvigsen2022grace}; the implementation is ours): one wrapped Linear module
(\texttt{down\_proj} at layer 18 of 24, $\approx$0.75 depth), whose codebook keys are the input
activation at the \emph{last cloze token} --- the position that predicts the first answer
token\footnote{Our first implementation keyed on the answer-token position, which is absent
during generation; the diagnostic signature --- recall pinned at zero while recognition rose ---
is itself an instance of the readout hierarchy (\S\ref{sec:paraphrase}), and the corrected key
position (last prompt token) restored ceiling recall on the pilot.} --- and whose values are
learned output offsets applied whenever a query activation falls within a fixed deferral radius
$\varepsilon$ (auto-set to half the median distance between clean keys). One codebook entry per
fact; each value is optimized by Adam. Two simplifications relative to official GRACE are noted
plainly: $\varepsilon$ is fixed, and there is no split/expand of colliding entries --- full
GRACE additionally grows the codebook via deferral-radius adaptation and key splitting on
conflicts, and edits a layer chosen by causal tracing; none of these is implemented here, which is
why we write ``GRACE-style'' throughout and claim only the discrete key--value mechanism. The runs
were pre-registered on 2026-07-13, before any data, with four predictions; all four held, and
one finding was not predicted.

\textbf{Results} ($n{=}48$, probes every 2 writes, 3 seeds per condition). At a canonical budget
(100 steps per fact, lr 1.0): recall 42/45/43 (mean 43.3), recognition 46/45/47, firewall
$0.70\to0.70$ --- \emph{parity} with the best SGD arm (ewc+replay miss, 44.2), not superiority.
The losses trace to $\varepsilon$-ball \emph{key collisions}: the codebook ends with 42/46/43
entries for 48 facts, and facts merged into a single entry lose --- a qualitatively different
failure mode from weight interference. At a \emph{matched} budget (8 steps per fact): recall
1/0/0 against recognition 47/45/47 (seed-paired: 1234 recalls 1 and recognizes 47; 2025, 0/45;
777, 0/47) --- the extreme recognition-without-recall dissociation
quoted in \S\ref{sec:grace}. On the CounterFact stream (canonical budget): write-form recall
38/39/40, but paraphrase 7/12/7 ($\sim$18\%) vs.\ the SGD replay arm's 20/10/15 ($\sim$31\%,
Table~\ref{tab:cf}) --- worse generalization, though the seed bands overlap, so direction only.
The editor reaches SGD-parity only at $\sim$12$\times$ the write compute; at matched compute it
expresses nothing while recognizing nearly everything. Editors and SGD-into-LoRA occupy
different corners of a retention--generalization--cost space; neither dominates.

\section{D30 horizon: replication and by-day trajectories}\label{app:d30}

\paragraph{Replication.} An audit correctly flagged the 30-day full and markov numbers as
single-seed. We re-ran both at seed 2025: \textbf{full 713/720 and markov 32/720 --- identical
counts to seed 1234} --- with the firewall at $0.70\to0.70$ in both runs. With the existing
two-seed misslog arm (326/345), all three D30 horizon arms have $n{=}2$, which is still thin,
but the two extreme arms landing on identical integers is about as clean as a two-seed
replication gets.

\paragraph{By-day trajectories.} The full arm is flat at ceiling: every write-day retains
22--24/24 at the final probe, with recognition 720/720 --- no downturn anywhere in the month, so
the r32 core's streaming capacity exceeds the generator's 784 unique attributes. The error-gated
arm settles into the throttled regime quoted in \S\ref{sec:horizon}: an old-day plateau at
7--18/24 with intake of the newest $\sim$5 days squeezed to 0--9/24; at fixed budget the system
does not race to a cliff, it throttles intake to protect its stock, and final recognition is
still 639--646/720 ($\sim$89\%). The markov arm's window is exactly the last $\sim$2 days at
every horizon. Locating the true knee requires harsher ratios (more facts per night-step) or a
larger generator, both future work.
